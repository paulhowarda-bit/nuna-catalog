# 2026-11-15, worker metrics: what each scan did, how long it took, when one last succeeded (backlog 40)

What changed:
- `WorkerMetrics` (catalog-maintenance) records each scan with Micrometer, in Boot's registry: a counter for each of
  the 11 counts `ScanReport` keeps, `keel.maintenance.failures` by `kind` (9 kinds, `ScanReport.Failure`), the timer
  `keel.maintenance.scan` by `outcome`, and the gauge `keel.maintenance.scan.last.success`, when the last successful
  scan ended in seconds since the epoch by the worker's clock, `0` until one has. Every meter is registered when the
  worker starts; the only labels are `kind` and `outcome`.
- A scan succeeds when it read the whole work list. A job failing in it is counted by kind and does not fail the
  scan; a scan that throws is a failure, with what it counted before.
- `Worker` takes the `MeterRegistry` (Boot's, in `WorkerApplication`); `ScanReport.failed` takes a kind, and the
  stale-table case is now a failure of kind `table-changed` (its line was `report.failures.add`).
- New `docs/observability/worker-metrics.md`: every meter, its Prometheus name under the default OTLP translation,
  alert rules (worker not scanning, worker gone, purges failing, deletions failing, a job failing at every scan, scans
  slower than the interval, files outside a table) and how to add a meter, for items 46, 47 and 49.
  `catalog-maintenance/README.md` points to it and lists the new tests.
- `bites/D30/worker-maintains-another-table.patch` recorded again, the same break, against the new failure line.

Choices, not settled by the item: a scan succeeds when it read the whole work list, so one table failing every scan
does not page as a worker that does not scan (its kind's counter and its alert say so); the gauge reads `0` until the
first success and the alert takes `max_over_time` over a day, so a restart does not fire it and a crash loop does; the
gauge is plain seconds, since Micrometer's OTLP registry sends times in milliseconds (`TelemetryTest` checks the
timer's unit).
