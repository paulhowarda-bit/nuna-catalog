What changed:

- `POST /management/v1/catalogs/{catalog}/objects/{id}/refs/remove` takes a table or view, by id and in any state but
  purged, out of the refs its body names (`{"refs": [...]}`) or out of every ref that names it (`{"all-refs": true}`).
  It needs `manage-tags` on the object (403 with a denial; 404 for a caller holding nothing on it), and is refused
  under a legal hold (`legal_hold`) or a DR freeze (`dr_freeze`) on the object, a namespace above it or the catalog.
  It writes one `ref` record per ref left, with the new action `OBJECT_REMOVED` and the object in `tables`, through
  the outbox.
- `RefStore.removeFromRefs` deletes the object's `ref_entry` row in each ref and each branch's own pointer for it
  with its projection pieces, in one transaction, on PostgreSQL, CockroachDB, Oracle and MongoDB, and checks the
  hold and freeze again under the object's lock. It takes turns with ref creates on the catalog's row (SQL) or a new
  `trimmed` stamp on the catalog's document (MongoDB, whose create now cuts again when a removal landed during it).
- Once no ref names a dropped table, its purge claim goes through and its namespace can be dropped.
