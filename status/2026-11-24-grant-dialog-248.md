# 2026-11-24: One Grant access dialog for the Grants page and every Access tab (backlog 248)

Branch `claude/musing-greider-bbfe27`. This builds Atelier's design for granting from the console: one dialog,
`grant.js`. It opens from the Grants page head and from "Grant access" at the top of every Access tab, and replaces the
Grants page's form and each Access tab's "Grant on this <kind>". The dialog:

- picks a group from those the catalog knows (backlog 247's route, after an unaudited `/grants/check` for
  manage-grants on the catalog; free text otherwise), with each group's duties, and marks the configured grant-admin
  and operator groups instead of offering them;
- offers only the access that means something on the object (no create on a table or view, no write on a view), in
  words with the wire name;
- finds the object on the Grants page by a path search on the names route;
- before Grant, shows one live line from backlog 246's preview and `/effective`, debounced, with Grant off while the
  answer is refused or already held.

After a grant it shows the words and a link to the record on Audit's Grants chip, and marks the grant in the list
behind it.

Atelier walked the dialog and said it was ready after twelve changes, all made (backlog 248 lists them). The one
blocker was the dialog's foot sitting below the screen; the answer, Grant and Close now stay in view at its foot.
