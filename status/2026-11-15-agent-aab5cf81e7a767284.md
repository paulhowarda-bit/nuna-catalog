# 2026-11-15, the console's health page: catalog health from every server, and each table's health (backlog 48, D38)

What changed:
- Server, new package `keel.catalog.server.health`: `HealthMeters`, a meter registry bean of its own that keeps every
  count and histogram bucket since start (Boot records every meter in it, beside OTLP's registry or alone), with
  histogram buckets for the audit publish timer here only; `HealthWindow`, a reading each minute, the last day being
  now's reading less the newest a day old; `Figures`, the flat counts read from the registry and shaped against the
  SLOs (a p99 within its objective when 99 in 100 requests are); `Peers`, which resolves each `keel.health.peers` entry
  to every address and asks each in parallel with the caller's own token, giving why for any that gives nothing;
  `CatalogHealthRoutes`, `GET /management/v1/health/server` and `/management/v1/health/catalog`, platform operators
  only (refused with 403 and audited otherwise), answers deduplicated by each server's random id. No schema step, no
  new dependency.
- Helm chart: a headless Service, `<release>-keel-catalog-peers` (`templates/service-peers.yaml`, ready pods only), and
  `KEEL_HEALTH_PEERS=<that>.<namespace>.svc:8181` in the Deployment. `helm lint` passes; `helm template` renders both.
- Console: `health.js` (the Health button, `#p=health`): catalog health (alerts by kind, audit acknowledgements, vends,
  the three SLOs, the worker's scans, the servers), or for anyone else the refusal; beneath it the neediest tables the
  viewer may select, each linking to its table's new **Health** tab (`detail.js`): the latest assessment, its reasons,
  the last compaction. `app.js` routes the page; `api.js` gains four reads; `style.css` a few rules.
- Docs: new docs/observability/health-page.md.

Checked:

- `ConsoleHealthTest`, Selenium in Chrome against a fake second server and a silent third: an operator sees each

  sees no catalog health, only the tables they may read, neediest first, each opening its Health tab, and a hidden
  table's health refused; someone with no grant sees neither, both routes refuse them (403), and their token never
  leaves the server.
- Every console test after the last change (61958b8), `./gradlew :catalog-server:test` with ConsoleHealthTest,
  ConsoleBrowserTest, ConsoleSignInTest, ConsoleConfiguredTest and ConsoleTest: 20 run, none failed (3, 7, 2, 2, 6).

  `catalog-health-for-anyone` and `server-health-for-anyone` (ConsoleHealthTest#someoneWithNoGrantSeesNoCatalogHealthAndNoTable,
