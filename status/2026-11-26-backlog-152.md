# 2026-11-26, brokerage world 3: party, money movement and compliance, the CDC sources and their governance (backlog 152)

The work is on branch `worktree-backlog-152` (written 2026-11-27).

## What changed

- **The world** (demo-datagen, `world/BackOffice.java`, its own random source): parties, account roles, CIP checks,
  suitability profiles, beneficial owners and trusted contacts; payments, ACH returns and ACATS transfers, each the
  ledger's own (templates 30 to 35, 70 to 77, 82 to 85); a synthetic sanctions list in OFAC's SDN layout with
  screening of onboarding and every wire; AML monitoring (large wires, structuring) with alerts, cases and SARs;
  wash-trade surveillance; complaints. Seeded: three structuring cash deposits on played days 1 to 3, and a wire from
  list entry 9101 on day 2, blocked and never booked. 15 new feeds and topics.
- **Lake**: `brokerage.party` gains six tables, `brokerage.money_movement` and `brokerage.compliance` are new, and each
  versioned feed has a `<table>_current` view (`live/Current.java`).
- **SingleStore route**: the compliance copies go under `brokerage.compliance.singlestore`, through a per-table
  namespace in the change feed (`iceberg.namespace.<table>`, singlestore-change-feed).
- **CDC leg**: PostgreSQL `backoffice` (schemas party, money_movement, compliance) and Oracle
  `TREASURY.BANK_PAYMENTS` replace the toy tables; `Main cdc` (was `Main custody`) loads them and CockroachDB with only
  what changed (`live/Sources.java`); Debezium writes `history.postgres.<schema>.<table>` and
  `history.oracle.treasury.bank_payments`.
- **Governance** (demo.sh): classifications, regulated `money_movement` and `compliance`, owners, a legal hold on
  `compliance.aml_cases`; `party.beneficial_owners` lands unclassified through its own sink,
  `brokerage-lake-owners`, which the generator restarts once a steward classifies the table. No D20 flags (item 152
  asks Paul). lakehouse's grants are now per namespace (`lake_grants`), so only `demo-compliance` reads a
  compliance namespace.
- **cora**, in group `demo-compliance`: Keycloak, the console's token, CloudBeaver, kafbat (compliance topics only),
  PostgreSQL (schema compliance), Oracle and CockroachDB.
