# 2026-11-27: backlog 321, the brokerage world runs 24x7, with a scale dial

Branch `brokerage-24x7`.

- The world plays each business day as the window from 20:30 the business day before to 20:30, all of it dated that
  business day. Customers act at every hour of every day, holidays included (`Activity`). Trading runs on Nasdaq's
  24x5 sessions (`Session`), with off-regular limit orders in the most popular fifth of the securities and thinner
  bars and quotes. A trade after 21:00 is dated the next trading day. A `session` column is added on `orders`,
  `order_events` and `executions`.
- Money requests made while the payments systems are shut are recorded as `requested` and booked at the next 07:00
  with that business date, or `rejected`. The back office's batch runs after 20:00.
- The scale dial: `demo/demo.sh scale <n>` (1 to 50), `BROKERAGE_SCALE` on Deployment `brokerage`, and the history in
  the lake's `world.scale`. The firm onboards toward n times its accounts (at most 20,000) and multiplies every
  account's activity.
- Played's seed moved from 160 to 163 and LakeSchemasTest's from 5 to 6, since the old seeds no longer drew a split or
  an ACATS delivery in their days.
