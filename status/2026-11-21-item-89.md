logged `Unable to execute HTTP request: Network is unreachable`, not `PKIX path building failed`. The cause is the
pods' network, not the Job: pods resolve `host.docker.internal` to `192.168.65.254` and `fdc4:f303:9324::254` and
route no IPv6 (checked in a busybox pod), and HttpClient 5.6.4 (shaded in the Spark image's iceberg-aws-bundle 1.12.0)
does the TLS handshake inside its per-address loop and reports only the last address's error (`javap` of
`DefaultHttpClientConnectionOperator`). So the IPv4 refusal was dropped and the IPv6 address's error reported.
