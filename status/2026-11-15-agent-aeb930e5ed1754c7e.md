# 2026-11-15, the worker's scans are traces, and its calls to the catalog carry them (backlog 49)

What changed:
- `ScanSpans` (catalog-maintenance): each scan is a Micrometer Observation, `keel.maintenance.scan.run`, span
  `maintenance scan`, the trace's root, its `outcome` the scan timer's (`success` when the whole work list was read).
  Each part is `keel.maintenance.part`, span `maintenance <part>`: `work-list` (per page, `section` on the span),
  `table-load`, `snapshot-expiry`, `manifest-rewrite`, `compaction-assessment`, `compaction-launch`, `orphan-scan`,
  `purge`, `deletion`, `report` (`report` on the span: scan-start, scan-end, assessment, compaction). Attributes are
  `part`, `outcome`, `exception` (simple name) and those two; a failure's span records the exception's class only.
- `Worker.of` wraps the JDK client in `micrometer-java11`'s `MicrometerHttpClient` for the management calls and the
  token requests: a client span (`HTTP GET` and so on) under the part, `traceparent` sent, `uri` a route
  (`Management.route`: ids `{id}`, the catalog `{catalog}`, no query).
- Iceberg's REST client (table loads, commits, refreshes) gets the current span's `traceparent` from
  `BearerAuthManager`'s auth session (`TraceHeaders`, from Boot's `Tracer` and `Propagator`); no client span of its own.
- `WorkerApplication` passes Boot's `ObservationRegistry`, `Tracer` and `Propagator`; tests pass
  `ObservationRegistry.NOOP` and `TraceHeaders.NONE`.
- Docs: `docs/observability/worker-spans.md` (new), README.md, worker-metrics.md (the three new timers),
  catalog-maintenance/README.md; `micrometer-java11` 1.17.2 on install-at-work.md's artifact list.
