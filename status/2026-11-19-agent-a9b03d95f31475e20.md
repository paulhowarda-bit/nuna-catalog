# 2026-11-20, a DR reconcile step whose outcome is unknown waits within the retry budget when the store is down (backlog 69, core part)

When a reconcile step's `store.commit` throws `CommitOutcomeUnknownException`, `Reconcile.apply` now asks
`findCommit` through the commit engine's own asking loop, `CommitEngine.decide`, which is now static and
package-visible; the engine's own calls are unchanged. A `StoreUnavailableException` is asked again within the
engine's P10 bounds: at most 4 asks, backoff 100, 200, 400 ms (up to 10% jitter), none starting after 2 s. The 2 s
count from the step's start, so each step has its own budget. An outage longer than that throws the store's failure
out of the apply, as it did before. Any other `StoreException` is still thrown at once. `Reconcile` takes the engine's
`CommitSettings`, clock and sleeper. The test `Harness` has one sleeper, which its engine and `ReconcileTest` share.
`CommitEngine.java` was added to the item's `Touches:` line and to the claim.
