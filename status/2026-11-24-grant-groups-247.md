# 2026-11-24, the groups that hold grants in a catalog (backlog 247)

**Built** on `claude/grant-groups-247-615013`: what item 248's group picker reads.
- `GrantStore.listGrantGroups(catalogId, page)`: each group holding a grant anywhere in the catalog, by group, with
  every privilege it holds there once (`GrantGroup`). PostgreSQL and CockroachDB (`SqlGrants`) and Oracle
  (`OracleGrants`) page the groups in an inner keyset read on `grant_by_group` and join their privileges in the same
  read; MongoDB (`MongoGrants`) groups in one aggregation.
- `GET /management/v1/catalogs/{catalog}/grants/groups?pageSize=&pageToken=`, manage-grants on the catalog, as the
  export: `{"groups": [{"group", "duties", "privileges"}], "grant-admin-groups", "platform-operator-groups", "note",
  "next-page-token"}`. Duties are D18's, `read`, `data`, `grants`, `tags`, now public as `Separation.Duty`. The
  configured groups come on every page; `note` says the catalog knows groups only from its grants, not the directory.

Path and shape: build-plan.md, M7, "Who may call the management routes".
