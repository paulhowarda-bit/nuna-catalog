# 2026-11-19, findCommit waits within the retry budget when the store is down (backlog 8)

`CommitEngine.settle` asks `findCommit` again when it throws `StoreUnavailableException`, within the engine's P10
bounds: at most `maxAttempts` (4) asks per unknown outcome, none starting after `retryBudget` (2 s) from the request's
start, with the commit loop's backoff (100, 200, 400 ms, up to 10% jitter). An outage longer than that answers as
before, 500 CommitStateUnknownException with the same message; any other `StoreException` still answers at once.
Commit, create, register and the multi-table transaction all settle there. `CommitSettings`' javadoc says the bounds
cover these asks. Nothing outside `catalog-core` changed.
