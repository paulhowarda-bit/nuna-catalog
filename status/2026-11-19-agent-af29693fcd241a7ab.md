# 2026-11-19, the maintenance worker's chart installed on Docker Desktop's Kubernetes (backlog 5, D30)

The `nuna-maintenance` chart was installed for the first time, unchanged, beside the catalog's chart on Docker
Desktop's Kubernetes. The worker had its own identity everywhere:
- at the identity provider: a Keycloak client with the client-credentials grant, issuer
  `http://keycloak:8080/realms/nuna`, azp `nuna-maintenance`, groups `[nuna-workers]`, scope `nuna-catalog`;
- in the catalog: a maintenance principal;
- in Silo: its own user, holding the catalog's `to` delete policy;
- in Kubernetes: its own service account, with its compaction Jobs in a namespace of their own.

It ran in namespaces `nuna-item5`, `nuna-item5-maintenance` and `nuna-item5-compaction`. Worker pod
`nuna-maintenance-54879d54f4-5cgjq` Running; first scan 04:45:12 UTC, "Scan done: tables 0, ... failures 0".

A Spark pod writing as `lake` made `lakehouse.item5.events` from six small files, and its maintenance policy was
set by `PUT .../maintenance-policy` (HTTP 200). The worker then scored the table "1.20 [SMALL_FILES]" and started
Job `nuna-compact-63aaecaf-442a-454d-9318-0bed5ba5d0c6-20261119` in `nuna-item5-compaction`. The Job finished
Complete 1/1 in 26 s, with "COMPACTED rewritten_data_files_count=6 added_data_files_count=1 ...", and the table's
current snapshot is a replace (1 data file, 12 records). The worker logged "The compaction Job of table events
(...) succeeded". The next scan ran an orphan scan with 0 orphans and 0 failures.

What the install found, each fixed:
- **The smoke check wrote as `trino`.** Since ea1d7a1 that user is an engine, so the catalog refused with 403. Its
  writes now go as `lake`.
- **Pods outside the catalog's namespace could not reach the store.** The catalog vended `http://silo:9000`, and
  every worker scan failed with `UnknownHostException: silo`. `deploy/local/run.sh up` now sets the profile's
  endpoint to `silo.<namespace>.svc`. The worker's URIs are set the same way.
- **A failed orphan scan waited a whole night.** The worker recorded a table's orphan scan as tonight's before it
  ran, so a scan that failed was not tried again until the next night. `catalog-maintenance/README.md` promises the
  next scan.
  - `Worker.scanOrphans` now records the scan once it finishes.
  - `FailedOrphanScanTest` is new and needs only Java. It failed before the change and passes after.
  - Bite `bites/D30/orphan-scan-failure-recorded.patch` is new. `orphan-scan-cut-short-recorded.patch` was recorded
    again against the new code.
  - Live, with the worker's Silo policy detached: the scan failed with "AccessDeniedException ... 403" and
    "failures 1". With the policy attached again, the next scan in the same night showed "orphan scans 1 ...
    failures 0".
- **A failing smoke left its port-forwards running.** `set -e` exits without the RETURN trap. The port-forwards are
  now stopped on EXIT.

New in `deploy/local`: `keycloak.yaml`, `worker-values.yaml`, a second issuer and `maintenance-principals` in
`values.yaml`, and `run.sh worker`. `run.sh down` now also deletes the namespaces. `deploy/README.md`,
`catalog-maintenance/README.md` and `docs/install-at-work.md` say how.

Afterwards, `run.sh down` removed both releases and all three namespaces:
- `helm list -A` is empty;
- only the four system namespaces remain;
- no pod runs outside `kube-system`.

The images `nuna-catalog:local` and `nuna-maintenance:local` remain in the local engine.
