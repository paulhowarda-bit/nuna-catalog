# 2026-11-25, the demo's metrics switch: Grafana, Prometheus and Tempo (backlog 233, D50 amended)

`demo/demo.sh metrics` starts an OpenTelemetry collector 0.166.0, Prometheus 3.16.0 (OTLP receiver, names pinned,
native histograms off, a 30-minute out-of-order window, exemplars kept, `deploy/grafana/rules` loaded), Tempo 2.10.10
and Grafana 13.3.2 on port 8188 (`demo/k8s/metrics.yaml`), and points the catalog's and the worker's
`telemetry.endpoint` at the collector, sampling every trace; `metrics stop` stops the four and points them away. Grafana
signs in through Keycloak's new public client `grafana` (PKCE); operators and presenters administer it. Prometheus,
Tempo and Grafana keep their data on volumes. `metrics` refuses without 1.5 GB of Docker Desktop's memory free; `ss`
refuses beside it without 1.5 GB. Paul added Tempo to the item.

Checked live, from the branch, on the running demo (gen and kafka on): `metrics` brought up the four pods and port
8188; 299 series names arrived, from `nuna-demo/nuna-catalog` and `nuna-demo/nuna-maintenance`; each of the three
dashboards' queries ran against them (scratch script, every panel target with the variables as `.*`): every name
held, and the panels without data are those that need a failure, the audit's Kafka acknowledgement (the demo audits to
its log) and the client reports' removals and delete files (append-only engines), as
`docs/observability/dashboards-and-alerts.md` now says. Three loads of a missing table showed in "Failed loads".
Tempo held traces from both services, a worker scan as one trace with hundreds of the catalog's spans in it (its calls, store and object-store work); exemplars
carry `trace_id`. oscar, ana and presenter signed in to Grafana through Keycloak (Admin, Viewer, Admin), each seeing
the three dashboards; both data sources answer healthy; all 19 rules loaded, the six SLO burn-rate alerts firing
rightly (75% of loads within 50 ms while Windows paged at about 700 MB free). `metrics stop` removed the pods and the
catalog's and worker's endpoint, and kept the three volumes. Fast build green after merging main.

Found on the way: Grafana 13 stalled for minutes at a 384 MiB limit (now 768 MiB); the first connection to a new
LoadBalancer port is often reset (retried); the catalog's first restart failed to reach PostgreSQL while Windows
paged ("cannot tell which database the store is on") and the second started.

Not covered here: `up` putting the switch back (checked after landing, by a rebuild from `main`), the switch beside the
CDC leg, and Grafana in a browser (the built-in browser refused the demo's certificate; curl drove the sign-in).
