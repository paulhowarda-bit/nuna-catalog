# 2026-11-27, a scan names each failed job's table and why (backlog 327)

- The worker's scan report carries `failed-jobs` beside the `failures` counts: at most 20 per scan, each with its kind,
  table (catalog, namespace, name, id), a reason (`closed`, `refused`, `not-found`, `changed`, `error`) and the message
  it met, cut to 300 characters.
- The catalog keeps them. `FailedJob` is new, and `MaintenanceScan.failedJobs` holds them. The stores needed a new
  nullable column, `failed_jobs` (PostgreSQL V20, CockroachDB V19, Oracle V22), and MongoDB a new field. The scans
  route takes and returns them.
- The Worker scans panel on Service and Maintenance names the last scan's failed tables, each linked. The Overview and
  Maintenance headlines name the tables not assessed yet.
