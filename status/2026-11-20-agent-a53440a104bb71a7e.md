# 2026-11-20, a storage profile's CA bundle reaches the audit archive and compaction Jobs (backlog 87, 88)

Item 21 let a storage profile name a CA bundle that the profile's S3 and STS clients trust beside the JVM's CAs. Two
clients were left out; both trust it now. Without a bundle nothing changes.

- Item 87, the server: `GovernanceWiring.archive` reads the archive profile's `ca-bundle` and gives the archive's
  S3 client the trust `CatalogStorage` gives the profile's other clients (`CaBundle.read`, `applyTo`). A bundle it
  cannot read stops startup with `nuna.storage.profiles.<profile>.ca-bundle: ` and the file, as `CatalogStorage`
  does; until now the archive's startup `checkBucket` failed with a TLS handshake error on such a store.
- Item 88, the worker, to Paul's choice of 2026-11-20 (D21 amended: "Worker mounts the CA"): a compaction Job for a
  table whose `storage` profile (the one whose prefixes hold its location, longest first, as `Storage` finds it)
  names a `ca-bundle` gets that CA. The Jobs run in a namespace of their own, and a pod mounts only ConfigMaps of its
  own namespace, so the worker cannot mount the chart's `storageCaConfigMap` itself: it copies the bundle it read
  into a ConfigMap of the Job's own, one certificate per key (`ca-0.pem`, ...), of the Job's Secret's fresh name,
  created after the Job and owned by it like the Secret, and mounts it at `/etc/nuna/storage-ca`. The Job's command
  then builds `/tmp/storage-trust.p12` from the image's JDK CAs (`$JAVA_HOME/lib/security/cacerts`) and the
  bundle's with keytool (quiet unless it fails, which stops the Job with keytool's output), and spark-submit gives
  the driver `-Djavax.net.ssl.trustStore` and its password in `spark.driver.extraJavaOptions`. Why this way and not
  the AWS SDK's trust settings: Iceberg's S3FileIO exposes no TLS trust property (only a client factory, which would
  need the worker's `TrustedS3Clients` on the Spark image's classpath), and the JVM trust store needs nothing in the
  image but the JDK, and covers the Job's every client (S3FileIO on vended credentials, the REST catalog client).
  The options go on spark-submit's command line because `compact.py` builds its session after the driver JVM has
  started. A table on a profile without a bundle gets exactly the Job it got before.
- `KubernetesJobs` takes the worker's storage profiles and reads their bundles as it is built, so one it cannot read
  stops the worker starting (`CaBundle.read` names the file). `Worker.of` passes them; the heavy
  `KubernetesCompactionTest` and the overnight `MatrixCompaction` pass none.
- Chart: the Role in the Jobs' namespace also grants `create` on ConfigMaps, only when a profile under
  `config.nuna.maintenance.storage` names a `ca-bundle`. Docs: `catalog-maintenance/README.md` ("Compaction on
  need", the settings table, the identity in Kubernetes), the chart's `values.yaml` and `rbac.yaml` comments,
  `docs/install-at-work.md` sections 5 and 6 (the image no longer has to trust the CA itself).
