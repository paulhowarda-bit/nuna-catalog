# 2026-11-19, the audit publish timer is exported with buckets, and the catalog dashboard shows its p99 (backlog 71)

The exported `nuna.audit.publish` timer had no histogram buckets, so Prometheus got only `_count` and `_sum` and item
44's dashboard could show only the mean Kafka acknowledgement time. Now `TracingWiring` (catalog-server, `tracing/`)
adds a meter filter bean giving the timer buckets at `ObservedPublisher.BUCKETS`: 5, 10, 25, 50, 100, 250 and 500 ms,
1, 2.5 and 5 s, the console health page's. Boot adds a meter filter bean to every registry it configures, the OTLP one
included. A registry that already gives the timer buckets of its own keeps them, so the health page's registry is as
it was. The timer is made by the observation handler, not by nuna's code, so a filter rather than the timer's own
configuration; `management.metrics.distribution.slo` would have meant `application.yaml`, outside the claim.

`deploy/grafana/dashboards/nuna-catalog.json` gets "p99 acknowledgement time, by event" (panel 38) beside the mean:
`histogram_quantile(0.99, sum by (event, le) (rate(nuna_audit_publish_milliseconds_bucket{...}[5m])))`. The records
and failures panels share the row below, so nothing else moved. dashboards-and-alerts.md, spans.md and health-page.md
say so; the p99 is an estimate within its bucket, and a p99 past 5 s reads 5 s.
