# 2026-11-19, the catalog list shows only the catalogs the caller holds something on (backlog 20)

`GET /management/v1/catalogs` (`UiRoutes.catalogs`) now lists a declared catalog only when one of the subject's
groups holds a grant, any privilege, on the catalog or on anything in it (`GrantStore.listGrantsOfGroups`, one row
asked per catalog), as D18's amendment of 2026-11-19 says. Grant admins and platform operators see every catalog; the
operators' answer is today's, kept until Paul decides (item 20's question). A caller who holds nothing gets an empty
list. The console's empty-list message changed to match. Nothing outside `catalog-server/` changed in code;
`build-plan.md`'s two sentences about the unfiltered list are corrected.
