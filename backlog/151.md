Second phase of D48 (`docs/design/brokerage-world.md`). The ledger's fills come from somewhere: an order management
system and a clearing house.

What to build:
- In SingleStore `ops`, replacing the toy `ops.orders`: orders, order events, routes and executions, consistent with
  the ledger's fills (same fill ids, prices, quantities), copied to `brokerage.trading` by the change feed; securities
  and closing prices loaded back from Iceberg by Iceberg pipelines.
- `brokerage.clearing`: CNS obligations per security and settlement date, settlements, fails, the stock record by
  location, locates (the kit's `locateId` joins them to short sales). Backfilled to Iceberg, and loaded into
  CockroachDB `custody` for the CDC leg in place of the toy `positions.holdings`.
- `brokerage.recon` views for ties 1, 3 and 4 of the design.
- Seeded break: one fail to deliver aged into a Reg SHO 204 close-out.

What done looks like: on a clean world the three views return no rows, and with the seeded break exactly the seeded
rows, read in Trino as `ana`; an `UPDATE` to an order in SingleStore reaches `brokerage.trading.orders` as before.

## What was done

The world (demo-datagen) now has an order management system and a clearing house beside the ledger, the lake has the
three reconciliation views, and the CDC leg's CockroachDB holds the clearing books in place of `positions.holdings`.
Recorded in `status/2026-11-26-brokerage-world-2.md`.

- **OMS** (`world/Oms.java`): orders, order events, routes and executions drawn from the ledger's fills (templates 41,
  44, 61 and 64), each execution carrying its fill's id, price and quantity. It draws nothing at random, so every other
  feed is the same as before. In SingleStore `brokerage`, `brokerage_oms_to_lake` copies the order events, routes and
  executions append-only into `brokerage.trading` in one pipeline (`demo/singlestore/feeds.sql`); orders keep their
  upsert change feed.
- **Clearing** (`world/Clearing.java`): CNS obligations, settlements, fails, the stock record and locates, sent on
  both routes (D50) like every numbered feed: a world already running sends them from its first day. The stock record counts fills, ACATS in and out (83, 84) and
  splits (86) with cash in lieu (87), so tie 1 holds through them.
- **Seeded break**: one net delivery holding a customer short sale fails, ages, is closed out under Reg SHO 204(a) at
  09:30 the next business day by a purchase for the firm's own account, and is delivered when that purchase settles.
- **Recon** (`live/Recon.java`, views created by `IcebergLake.reconViews`): `brokerage.recon.stock_record_vs_ledger`
  (tie 1), `executions_vs_fills` (tie 3) and `cns_vs_fills` (tie 4). They read both routes' tables and only days
  before `current_date`. On a clean world all three are empty. On the seeded world only `cns_vs_fills` has a row: the
  close-out, a purchase that no customer fill holds.
- **Custody** (`live/Custody.java`, `Main custody`): the CockroachDB tables `custody.clearing.stock_record`,
  `cns_obligations`, `fails` and `locates`. The job is run by `demo.sh cdc` through the bootstrap job; it replays the
  world to the lake's last end of day and writes only what changed. Debezium captures them into
  `history.cockroach.clearing.*`, whose tables `demo.sh` creates from `demo/cdc/custody-history/`.
- **demo.sh**:
  - retires `ops`;
  - frees world 1's `brokerage.trading.order_events` for the change feed;
  - classifies `brokerage.clearing` and `brokerage.recon` confidential and `brokerage.kafka` internal;
  - adds the clearing login;
  - restarts a CDC leg begun before this item, once;
  - waits for the custody history rows.

Choices made here, each one Paul may overrule:

- **Where the OMS lives**: SingleStore database `brokerage`, beside `brokerage.orders`, as the design's table puts
  it, not `ops` as this item says. `ops` and its toy pipeline are retired: `demo.sh` drops pipeline
  `ops.orders_to_lake` and database `ops` where they exist. A choice made here, Paul may overrule.
- **When SingleStore is off**:
  - The OMS rows go through topics `brokerage.oms.*` to `brokerage.kafka.<table>`.
  - The orders' upserts go nowhere, since each change to an order is also an order event.
  - World 1's topic `brokerage.trading.order-events` is retired. Its table `brokerage.trading.order_events` is deleted,
    with its files, if it is not the change feed's.
  - A choice made here, Paul may overrule.
- **Expired event**: an order the close cancels (templates 80 and 81) ends with event `expired`. A choice made here,
  Paul may overrule.
- **Clearing model**: a self-clearing broker in CNS, T+1. There is one obligation per security and trade date,
  settling at 08:00 on its settlement date. A choice made here, Paul may overrule.
- **Stock record**:
  - Owners are customer (per account) and firm.
  - Locations are `dtc`, `stock_borrow`, `cns_pending` and `fail_to_deliver`. With X = owned − pending + failed,
    `dtc` = max(X, 0) and `stock_borrow` = min(X, 0).
  - Quantities are decimal(18, 5): an ACATS transfer or a split can leave a fraction until cash in lieu.
  - A choice made here, Paul may overrule.
- **Locates**: on the easy-to-borrow list at general collateral, 0.25% a year, unless the world makes the security
  hard to borrow (item 155's rule). Those come from the stock loan desk at the world's rate. A choice made here, Paul
  may overrule.
- **Seeded fail**: on the world's third business day or later, the first net delivery holding a customer short sale
  fails for the short sale's shares (borrow not received). The fail's quantity is not rescaled by a split while it is
  open; the seeded world has none. A choice made here, Paul may overrule.
- **Governance**: `brokerage.clearing` and `brokerage.recon` are confidential, owned by clearing-operations.
  `brokerage.kafka` is internal, owned by trading-desk. A choice made here, Paul may overrule.
- **Custody contents**: the latest day's stock record, five days of CNS obligations and locates, and each fail at its
  latest state, with its close-out kept once delivered. Settlements are not loaded: they are events, in the lake. Rows
  are loaded by the job at each `cdc`; `demo.sh` writes no DML to CockroachDB any more. A choice made here, Paul may
  overrule.
- **CDC leg**: a leg begun before this item has no `custody=clearing` in configmap `cdc-begun`, so it starts anew once
  and its kept volume loses the old `positions.holdings`. A choice made here, Paul may overrule.
- **Test seed**: the tests play seed 160, whose two weeks hold ACATS in and out and a split. A choice made here, Paul
  may overrule.
