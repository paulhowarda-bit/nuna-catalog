Each table's Metadata log tab lists its metadata versions, newest first, a page at a time (D45). Today the only
way to get the journal is whole, from `GET .../objects/{id}/export`.

What done looks like: a paged read of a table's or view's metadata log, with a page token, and a route test.

## Done

`GET /management/v1/catalogs/{catalog}/objects/{id}/metadata-log?pageSize=&pageToken=`, beside the export in
`governance/LifecycleRoutes.java`, not `rest/ObjectRoutes.java`: the export lives there. It needs select on the
object, as the export does. It answers the export's journal entries newest first, 25 a page by default and at most
100, with the pointer and a `next-page-token`. A page is one bounded read of the journal the store already keeps; the
store is unchanged. Tests: `MetadataLogPageTest` (fast), two in `LifecycleTest` and one in
`ManagementAuthorizationTest` (heavy). Bite: `bites/D45/metadata-log-open.patch`. The route is documented in
`build-plan.md`'s M7 table, below the export. Status: `status/2026-11-22-metadata-log-pages.md`.
