What is wrong: `demo/demo.sh up` starts everything but the CDC leg: Kafka (640 MiB), Kafka Connect (1 GiB), kafbat
(768 MiB), the brokerage world's generator (384 MiB) and SingleStore (3 GiB), whether or not the demo uses them. Paul,
2026-11-23: "Lets have 2 swithch cdc turns on and off cdc. Gen turns on an off the generator, ss turns on an off
singlestore", and Kafka only where it is needed: "So kafka is needed for the live data generator. And cdc." (D50.)

Done looks like:
- `demo.sh gen` / `gen stop` and `demo.sh ss` / `ss stop` beside `cdc` / `cdc stop`. Kafka, Kafka Connect and kafbat
  run only while `cdc` or `gen` is on, and stop when the last of them stops.
- `up` starts the core (PostgreSQL, Keycloak, Silo, the issuer's key server, the catalog with no Kafka, the audit table
  writer fed over OTLP, Trino, CloudBeaver, the front door), turns `gen` and `ss` back on if they were, kept in
  `~/.nuna-demo/switches` (all off on a new machine), and keeps a running CDC leg. What a switch needs at setup (SingleStore's databases and loads,
  the brokerage world's topics and sink) runs when it is turned on.
- `gen` turns `ss` on (the generator writes orders to SingleStore) and `ss stop` turns `gen` off; `engines` and
  `sql` refuse, naming the switch, when what they need is off.
- `print_access`, `status` and `demo/README.md` show which switches are on and list only what runs.
- Checked by a rebuild with every switch off, then each switch on and off, the SSO check passing throughout.
