branch."

The only way to a catalog's branches and tags is the header's "Reading main" control, which reads as a status label,
then its footer link "Branches and tags of lakehouse" (`#c=<catalog>&tab=refs`), then "Make a branch or tag"
(`refs.js`, `data-act='ref-new'`). The left rail (Overview, Catalog, Maintenance, Governance, Audit, Service) has no
entry for it. The refs list is open to anyone who holds anything in the catalog; making one needs `create`.

Done when the rail has an entry for branches and tags for anyone who may read them, where and as Atelier decides, with
"Make a branch or tag" on that page for those who hold `create`; the Reading control stays. Other sections reachable
only through a menu, as Atelier names them, are fixed here if small, else filed. A browser test checks the rail entry
and that it opens the page with the button for a creator and without it for one who may not create; the bite is
recorded. Checked live on the demo as a user who may create and one who may not.
