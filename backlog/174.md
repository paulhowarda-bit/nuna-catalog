What is wrong: the console, kafbat, Studio, CloudBeaver and Keycloak's account page sign in through Keycloak,
but two UIs take passwords of their own:

- Silo's console (8184) offers only its access-key form: the store's root, `nunatest`.
- Keycloak's admin console (8180/admin) takes the master realm's `admin` and password only.

Done when one person who typed a password once, at the console, opens every UI they have rights in without
typing it again, and is refused where they have none; checked in the running demo for `ana` and `oscar`, and
`demo/README.md` says who gets in where. Kafka Connect's REST API (8186) and Silo's S3 API (8187) are APIs, not
UIs, and are out of scope.

Choices made (Paul may overrule):

- Silo's console signs in the platform operators (`demo-operators`) with Silo's built-in policy `diagnostics`:
  the server's health, metrics, logs and traces, and no bucket or object, so they see no data in the store, as
  in the catalog (D18). The analysts, grant admins and stewards are refused: reading the store directly would go
  round the catalog's grants, governance and audit. `nunatest` keeps its access-key sign-in.
- Keycloak's admin console: the operators read realm `nuna` there (view roles only) through single sign-on.
  Changing the realm stays with the master realm's `admin`: whoever changes it can put themselves in any group.

admin console signs the operators in with realm-management's view roles. `up` now waits for Silo's rollout before
the seed makes the bucket. Checked in the demo rebuilt from the branch: oscar signs in to all 7 UIs after one
