Paul compared the live console with Atelier's prototype on 2026-11-24 and found it doesn't look like it; one of two
causes is the header ("fix both").

`app.js` `showWho` writes every group by name after the user's name: for `presenter`, "demo-analysts,
demo-auditors, demo-grant-admins, demo-operators, demo-stewards". At 1440 px wide the page is then 1,687 px
(`document.documentElement.scrollWidth`): the Light/Dark switch and Sign out are pushed off-screen behind a
horizontal scrollbar. Atelier's prototype (`docs/design/console/prototype.html?user=presenter`) shows
"presenter · 4 groups · until 18:42".

Done when the header reads "<name> · <n> groups · until <time>" as the prototype does, with the group names on hover
and on keyboard focus, and the page is never wider than the window at 1440 px or at a narrow width for a user in
many groups. The menu drawer's "Signed in as …" line keeps the full list. A browser test checks the words, the
names on the groups and that nothing scrolls sideways; its bite is recorded. Checked live on the demo as presenter.
