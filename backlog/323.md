Effort: M, a read route or a change to one, its authorization and audit, and the Governance page for an operator

A platform operator may place a DR freeze and lift one (`requireOperatorOn`), but may not list them:
`listHoldsAndFreezes` needs `describe` (`GovernanceRoutes.java:127`), which an operator holds nowhere. So a freeze he
did not place himself, or whose path he forgot, stays in force unseen, and a freeze refuses every commit beneath it.
The 27 Nov console review (item 3) asked; Atelier recommended yes, for DR freezes only.

Paul's decision, 2026-11-27 (D55): yes.

- A platform operator reads the standing DR freezes in a catalog: each one's path, reason, who placed it and when.
- Legal holds stay behind `describe` (or `manage-tags`): their reasons are legal matter.
- Before it is built, catalog-security confirms that listing the frozen paths to an operator is acceptable (he can
  already type any path to place or lift one), and says what the read records in the audit trail.
- The console's Governance page shows an operator the freezes he may lift, with Lift beside each.

Done when the route lets an operator list the freezes and refuses him the holds, tested and audited, and the
Governance page lists them for him.


What was done: catalog-security approved it on 2026-11-28 with the rule built here, recorded in D55. A new route,
`GET /management/v1/catalogs/{catalog}/governance/dr-freezes` (`listDrFreezes`, `GovernanceRoutes.drFreezes`), asks
`requireOperatorOn` on the catalog and answers `{"catalog", "freezes": [{"on", "reason", "placed-by", "placed-at"}]}`,
newest first. It reads through `GovernanceSummary.readFreezes`, the walk and its attributes with no hold read, and
`drFreezes()`, which builds each node as `standing()` builds a freeze, less its `kind`: it never calls `standing()`,
`holdsOn` or `HoldHistory`, and carries no lift, count or closed table. A grant holder who is no operator gets 403 and a
`no_privilege` denial; a caller holding nothing in the catalog gets the hidden catalog's 404 from `Holdings.catalog`; a
read records nothing. `/governance/holds` is unchanged and still refuses an operator.

The console's operator Governance page (`freezePage`) fills `#operator-freezes`: a Standing panel with each freeze's
path, reason (a text node, never markup), when and by whom, and Lift through the existing `drFreeze` dialog, or "No DR
freeze stands in <catalog>." An operator who also holds grants sees the full page as before: with describe its
standing list already has Lift beside each freeze, so no second list is drawn there. Documented in
`docs/site/api/governance.html`, `api/index.html`, `console/governance.html`, `guides/freeze.html` and build-plan.md's
route table. Fast tests in `GovernanceSummaryTest` (4 new, 14 green); heavy cases in `GovernanceSummaryRouteTest` and
`ConsoleBrowserTest` written but not run here (no container engine). Bites in `bites/D55/`, both run by hand and biting.
