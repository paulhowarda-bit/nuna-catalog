# 67. An unregister whose outcome is unknown waits within the retry budget when the store is down

Item 8 made `findCommit` wait within the retry budget (P10: at most 4 attempts or 2 s) when the store cannot be
reached, so a commit, create, register or transaction whose outcome is unknown during a short outage is settled. An
unregister does not settle through `findCommit`: when `unregisterObject` throws `CommitOutcomeUnknownException`, the
engine reads the object back with `readObject`, and when that read fails it answers 500 CommitStateUnknownException
at once (`CommitEngine.unregister`, `catalog-core/src/main/java/nuna/catalog/core/commit/CommitEngine.java` about
line 650), whatever the failure. Item 8 left it alone, as its title names `findCommit`.

`Reconcile.apply` (the DR runbook's reconcile, D37) also asks `findCommit` after an unknown outcome, with no wait: a
store outage there throws out of the apply to the operator's route at once. Nothing is lost (a second run plans
again from the pointer), but the count of steps already applied is not reported. Giving it the wait means passing
the engine's `CommitSettings` and sleeper to `Reconcile`, which `catalog-server`'s `ReconcileRoutes` constructs;
it is noted here, not part of this item.

Done when an unregister whose outcome is unknown during a short outage is settled within the budget, shown by a fault
test in `catalog-core` as item 8's are (`CommitEngineTest$UnknownOutcomes`), and an outage longer than the budget
still answers 500 CommitStateUnknownException.

Done: when `unregisterObject` throws `CommitOutcomeUnknownException`, the engine reads the table back as it did,
but a read that fails with `StoreUnavailableException` (the store cannot be reached) is asked again within the
engine's P10 bounds, as item 8's `findCommit` asks: at most `CommitSettings.maxAttempts` (4) reads, with the commit
loop's backoff (100, 200, 400 ms, up to 10% jitter), none starting more than `retryBudget` (2 s) after the request
started. The loop item 8 wrote in `CommitEngine.settle` is now `CommitEngine.decide`, which `settle` and the
unregister both call, so there is one copy. When the store answers, the unregister is settled as before: landed is a
success, not landed is sent again if the budget allows. An outage longer than the budget answers exactly as before,
500 CommitStateUnknownException with the same message; any other `StoreException` from the read still answers at once.

Tests: `UnregisterOutageTest` (new, 7 cases) runs the engine's unregister against a store double that holds one
table in memory, so it needs no database and runs in the fast build: the store up, a short outage, a 600 ms outage
after which the unregister is sent again, an outage longer than the budget (the same 500 after 4 reads), 10 attempts
allowed (no read starts after 2 s: 5 reads), an unregister that hung for 1.95 s (one read), and a store that answers
but cannot say (one read). The same cases, five of them, run on PostgreSQL in `CommitEngineTest$UnknownOutcomes`
(`unregister...`), tagged heavy like the rest of that class. Bite `bites/M8/unregister-read-back-answers-at-once.patch`
recorded and run by hand through the fast build: the three cases it names and `readsStopAtTheTimeBudget` fail on
assertions, the other three pass, and `CommitEngine.java` hashed as committed after it was reverted. Item 8's
`findcommit-answers-at-once` and `unknown-commit-answers-503` recorded again, the same breaks, against `settle`'s new
shape.
