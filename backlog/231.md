  6. The Catalog and Reading labels at 1,100 px and below.
  7. Sign out's words and the / hint at 900 px and below.

  note "This page shows main only" wraps onto two lines.

  1,100 px it was just "/"), and the avatar is named "Signed in as …, n groups, until …" while the who-text is hidden.
  The phone header (760 px and below) is as it was.
