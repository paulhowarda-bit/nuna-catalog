## What is wrong

Since item 181 (D48, amended 2026-11-23, "Real clock, quiet off-hours") the world plays only business days, every
event between 07:00 and 17:30 New York time (`World.runDay`): orders 09:31 to 15:31, deposits, withdrawals and
journals 08:30 to 16:30, the digital clickstream 07:00 to 16:59. Nights, weekends and holidays are silent, which no
retail broker is.

## What done looks like

- **Customers act around the clock, every calendar day, holidays included**: app and web sessions (Digital), logins,
  profile, address, contact, trusted-contact and suitability changes, deposit, withdrawal, wire, journal and ACATS
  requests, complaints. Activity follows a believable curve by hour and weekday (evenings and weekends busy for
  retail, a trickle at 03:00), not a flat rate.
- **Trading follows Nasdaq's announced schedule** (Equity Trader Alert 2026-46, effective Sunday, December 6, 2026):
  from 21:00 ET Sunday to 20:00 ET Friday, with an industry-wide pause from 20:00 to 21:00 each weekday; the sessions
  are overnight (21:00 to 04:00), pre-market (04:00 to 09:30), regular (09:30 to 16:00) and post-market (16:00 to
  20:00). Orders outside the regular session are limit orders, marked with their session; volume and quote width follow
  the session (thin and wide overnight). A trade between 21:00 and midnight takes the next trading day's trade date;
  from midnight to 20:00 the day's own. Settlement goes by trade date (T+1), as now. No trading Friday 20:00 to Sunday
  21:00, nor on exchange holidays (the session that would open the evening before a holiday does not).
- **The books keep business days**: money moves when the systems that move it run. A deposit or withdrawal asked for
  on a Saturday is recorded at once (the request, the customer's view) and booked, batched and settled on the next
  business day, with that business date; the ledger, CNS, the stock record, the reconciliation views and the
  `eod-YYYY-MM-DD` tags keep their business-day meaning and the recon views still return no rows on a consistent world.
- The back office's daily jobs run at the hours a firm would run them (the overnight batch after the 20:00 pause, say),
  not bunched at 07:00 and 17:30.
- The real clock still holds: catch-up days and today's earlier hours go at once, then each event at its own time.
  Nothing goes quiet: every hour of every day sends events.
- Tests: the generator's tests show events in every hour of a week, weekend and holiday customer activity, no trades in
  the weekend gap or the 20:00 pause, the trade date after 21:00, and weekend money requests booked on the next
  business day; the kit's golden and the engine's self-checks still hold. Each new guarded check records its bite.
- `docs/design/brokerage-world.md` and `demo/README.md` describe the 24x7 world.
- **A scale dial** (Paul, above): `demo/demo.sh scale <n>` (1 to 50; alone it prints the scale), kept in
  `~/.nuna-demo/` so `up` and `gen` put it back, changed on a running world with no reset (an environment variable on
  Deployment `brokerage`, whose pod restarts). At n the firm grows toward n times its accounts through the normal
  onboarding flows and each account's activity multiplies, so events per hour scale with n; turned down, onboarding
  stops and the rates fall, and no account is deleted. The world's persisted settings keep the scale over time, so a
  replay is the same. Tests: a higher scale gives proportionally more events an hour and more accounts over days, and
  a change mid-world keeps the books consistent.

## Done

2026-11-27, branch `brokerage-24x7`. Business day d is now played as the window from 20:30 on the business day before
to 20:30 on d, every event in it dated d. Customers act at every hour of every calendar day on an hour and weekday
curve (`Activity`), with at least one digital session in each half-hour. Trading follows Nasdaq's four sessions
(`Session`): Sunday 21:00 to Friday 20:00, the 20:00 pause each weekday, none on a holiday. Outside the regular session
only limit orders trade, in the most popular fifth of the securities, with thinner bars and wider quotes. A trade after
21:00 is dated the next trading day. `orders`, `order_events` and `executions` gain a `session` column (LakeSchemas,
SingleStore's DDL). Money requests made outside 07:00 to 19:00 on a business day (wires to 15:00) are recorded as
payments with status `requested` and booked at the next 07:00 with that business date, or `rejected`. The back office's
batch now runs after 20:00. The scale dial is `demo/demo.sh scale <n>`, kept in `~/.nuna-demo/scale` and passed as
`BROKERAGE_SCALE`; the generator keeps its history in the lake (`world.scale`) and applies a change from the next whole
hour. New tests: `World24x7Test` (Thanksgiving week), `ScaleDialTest`, a recon run on a world whose scale goes up and
then down (`ReconTest`), and the lake's round trip of the dial. Seven bites were added in `bites/WORLD-321/`, and six
that the change moved were recorded again. The fast build is green.

Not run live. A world begun before this change needs a world-only reset: its events changed, and three tables gained
a column.
