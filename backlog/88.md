Item 21 makes the worker's own S3 clients trust a profile's `ca-bundle`. A compaction Job (`KubernetesJobs`) is
Spark in its own pod, reading and writing the table with the credentials the catalog vends it, and it gets no
bundle: on an on-prem store whose certificate a private CA signed, it fails its first S3 call with a TLS
handshake error. Item 21 treats the Job as an engine, which trusts the store through its own setup, and
`docs/install-at-work.md` (section 6) says its image must trust the CA itself.

The choices, Paul's:

- Leave it to the image, as item 21's docs say: whoever builds the Spark image at work adds the CA to its JVM's
  trust store.
- Have the worker carry it: mount `storageCaConfigMap` into each Job's pod and point Spark's S3 clients at it, for
  example with `s3.client-factory-impl` and item 21's `TrustedS3Clients` on the image's classpath, or a JVM trust
  store built from the PEM at the pod's start.

Done when Paul has chosen and a compaction Job on a store with a private CA can read and write its table, or the
docs say plainly that the image must trust the CA.

**Decided 2026-11-20 (D21, amended):** "Worker mounts the CA": the maintenance worker mounts the profile's CA ConfigMap into each compaction Job and points Spark's trust at it.

Done, to Paul's choice (the worker carries the CA): a compaction Job for a table whose worker `storage` profile (the
one whose prefixes hold its location) names a `ca-bundle` trusts it; a table on a profile without one gets exactly
the Job it got before.
- The Jobs run in a namespace of their own, and a pod mounts only ConfigMaps of its own namespace, so a Job cannot
  mount the chart's `storageCaConfigMap` itself. `KubernetesJobs`, which now takes the worker's storage profiles and
  reads their bundles as it is built, copies the bundle into a ConfigMap of the Job's own, one certificate per key,
  named like the Job's Secret, owned by the Job and created after the Secret, and mounts it at
  `/etc/nuna/storage-ca`. The chart's Role in the Jobs' namespace grants `create` on ConfigMaps, only when a profile
  under `config.nuna.maintenance.storage` names a `ca-bundle`. Paul is asked whether this copy is what he meant.
- Spark's trust: the Job's command builds `/tmp/storage-trust.p12` from the image's JDK CAs
  (`$JAVA_HOME/lib/security/cacerts`) and the bundle's with keytool, quiet unless it fails (the Job then stops with
  keytool's output), and spark-submit passes `-Djavax.net.ssl.trustStore` and its password in
  `spark.driver.extraJavaOptions`. Chosen over the AWS SDK's own trust settings because Iceberg's S3FileIO exposes
  none (only a client factory, which would need the worker's `TrustedS3Clients` on the Spark image), while the JVM
  trust store needs nothing in the image but the JDK and covers every client the Job opens. The options go on
  spark-submit's command line: `compact.py` builds its session after the driver JVM has started.
- Docs: `catalog-maintenance/README.md` ("Compaction on need", the settings table, the identity in Kubernetes), the
  chart's `values.yaml` and `rbac.yaml` comments, `docs/install-at-work.md` sections 5 and 6 (the image no longer
  has to trust the CA itself).
- Tests (Java only): `KubernetesJobsTest#aStorageCaBundleIsMountedIntoTheJobAndSparkTrustsIt` (a bundle of two
  certificates: the ConfigMap after the Job and its Secret, its name, owner and one certificate per key; the volume,
  the mount and the trust-store command; a table on a profile without a bundle gets the plain Job and no ConfigMap),
  and the existing Job test now checks that without a bundle the command is the old one exactly, with no volume or
  mount. `helm template` of the chart with and without a `ca-bundle`. By hand, outside the build: the command's
  trust-store half, read from the compiled class, run with Git Bash and the local JDK, built a store of the JDK's
  153 CAs and the bundle's 2; a JVM given the driver's options then completed a handshake with the private CA's
  server where one without them failed; a broken certificate stopped it with keytool's error.
- Bites, recorded and checked with `git apply --check`, not run: `bites/M6/job-storage-ca-not-mounted`,
  `job-storage-ca-not-trusted` and `job-storage-ca-any-profile`.
- Left: no compaction Job has run with a bundle. The heavy `KubernetesCompactionTest` and the overnight engine
  matrix use no private CA and did not run tonight, so the Spark image's own keytool, `JAVA_HOME` and cacerts
  password are checked only by the hand run on a Windows JDK, not in the image.
