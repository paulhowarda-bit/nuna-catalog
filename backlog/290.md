What is wrong: since the move to Podman (item 287), the stores' fault tests fail at their first container engine
call with `NoHttpResponseException: localhost:2375 failed to respond`. In the gate of 2026-11-26: MongoDB's
failover test and its commit while failing over (kill), CockroachDB's failover test and its in-flight commit
(pause), Oracle's two log-writer tests (exec create).

Evidence: a probe on Podman 6.2.0 through `//./pipe/docker_engine` with Testcontainers' shared client: after a GET,
a POST (exec create) sent 1, 3, 6 or 9 s later succeeds; sent 12, 20 or 40 s later it fails in 2 to 5 ms with that
exception, and a GET sent as late succeeds. Podman's API closes a connection idle about 10 s; over the pipe the
client's pool does not see the close, and hands the dead connection to the next request. HttpClient retries a GET on
a new connection but not a POST, so the POST fails without reaching Podman. Docker Desktop kept idle connections
open. Kill, pause, unpause, start and exec are all POSTs, and a fault test is idle longer than 10 s between faults.

Done when the store harnesses make their engine calls over a fresh connection, and the failover cases of
`MongoStoreSuiteTest` pass on Podman with the primary really killed.

What was done: covered by item 291 instead, and this item's own fix was dropped (Paul chose the client fix,
2026-11-26). 291 gives Testcontainers' shared client a 5 s life for pooled connections on Windows, which fixes the
same POSTs in every module, where this item's per-call client (`ContainerEngine`) reached only the stores' fault
harnesses. On 291's branch, `MongoStoreSuiteTest` passed 226 of 226 on Podman with both failover cases and the
scratch server, and CockroachDB's failover and pause cases passed, with none of this item's code. Its branch's
commits (cede99fa, 51e8739a) were not landed.
