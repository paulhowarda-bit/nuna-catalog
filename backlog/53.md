# 53. Count credential vends served from the cache apart from new issues (after 41)

Status: open
Added: 2026-11-15 by backlog 39
Touches: catalog-server/src/main/java/keel/catalog/server/storage/StsCredentialVending.java catalog-server/src/main/java/keel/catalog/server/rest/CredentialVending.java catalog-server/src/main/java/keel/catalog/server/metrics/ catalog-server/src/test/java/keel/catalog/server/metrics/ docs/observability/catalog-metrics.md bites/OBS-39/

Starts once item 41 is on main: 41 holds `catalog-server/.../storage/`, where the cache is consulted.

Item 39 asked for credential vends counted with their cache hits (D21). `keel.catalog.credential.vends` times every
vend with outcome `issued`, `none` or `failed`, but a vend answered from the credential cache
(`StsCredentialVending.vend`, `cache.get(key, () -> mint(...))`, the cache in catalog-objectstore's `CredentialCache`)
counts as `issued` like a new STS session. Nothing outside `storage/` can tell the two apart: `CredentialVending.Vended`
does not say which it was.

Done when a vend served from the cache is told apart from one that minted new credentials, as an outcome of
`keel.catalog.credential.vends` (for example `cached` beside `issued`) or a hit and miss count beside it, with no new
label beyond item 39's. `CatalogMetricsRoutesTest` shows a first load minting and a second load hitting the cache,
`docs/observability/catalog-metrics.md` says so, and a bite in `bites/OBS-39/` counts a hit as a new issue.
