# 132. A refusal under a DR freeze is not audited

Effort: S, the refusal published as other refusals are, with a test and a bite

A request refused because a DR freeze covers its target answers 403, "... is frozen for DR reconcile: ...". The
refusal comes from `Management.requireUnfrozen` (Management.java, line 237), which `LifecycleRoutes` and
`ObjectRoutes` call. Unlike every other refusal, no denial record is published for it. Atelier found this while
building the prototype's audit feed, which shows the refusal as unaudited to match the server.

What done looks like:
- The refusal published as a `nuna.catalog.denial` record with its reason.
- A route test.
- A bite that drops the publish.

What was done (2026-11-22):
- Every refusal under a DR freeze is published as a `nuna.catalog.denial` record through `Denials`, as every other
  refusal is. Its reason is `dr_freeze: ` and then the message the caller gets, which carries the freeze's own reason.
  The answer is unchanged: 403 ForbiddenException, same message.
  - Undrop and purge claim: the check moved from `Management.requireUnfrozen` to `ManagementGuard.requireUnfrozen`,
    which holds `Denials`. The denial names the dropped table or view. The purge claim's second freeze refusal, when
    the store's own guard finds a freeze placed after the route looked (`WriteResult.Reason.FROZEN`), is audited too.
  - Drop and rename: `ObjectRoutes.requireUnfrozen` refuses through `Guard.refuse`, the path for an operation the
    caller's privileges allow but governance forbids. A rename into a frozen namespace names the table being renamed.
- The Touches line now names `ManagementGuard.java`, the two tests and the two bites. `./backlog.sh claim 132 <path>`
  cannot add paths to a claim already held; it failed with "already claimed" after its overlap check passed, so no
  other claim holds them.
- Side effect: drop and rename refusals now throw `AccessDenied` instead of a `CatalogError`, so an idempotency key no
  longer records the 403. A retry after the freeze is lifted runs afresh, as a retry after a grant does.
- Tests, in the fast build: `FrozenObjectRefusalTest` (drop, rename of a frozen table, rename into a frozen namespace)
  and `FrozenLifecycleRefusalTest` (undrop, purge claim, purge claim the store finds frozen).
- Bites: `bites/D18/dr-freeze-object-refusal-unaudited.patch` and `bites/D18/dr-freeze-lifecycle-refusal-unaudited.patch`
  each throw the 403 without publishing; `bites/run.sh` gave BITES (3 of 3 failed) for each, tree restored.
- Not here: a commit refused by a DR freeze (`Rules`, in `catalog-core`) and an unregister refused by one
  (`CommitEngine`) answer 403 without a denial, as do the legal-hold refusals of drop and purge claim. None of them is
  in this item.
