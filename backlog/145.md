Item 139's Store panel left point-in-time recovery out, saying the catalog cannot know it: `health.js` says
"Point-in-time recovery is not shown: it is the database's own backup setting, which the catalog cannot read", and
`docs/observability/health-page.md` and `backlog/139.md` say the same. But the deployment declares it:
`nuna.governance.pitr-window` (`GovernanceSettings.pitrWindow`, default 7 days), and the catalog's guardrails rely on
it. Orphan removal's older-than must exceed it (`MaintenancePolicy`), and a dropped table's retention must be longer
than it (`GovernanceWiring.requireDropWindowBeyondPitr`). Paul approved showing it on 2026-11-22.

What done looks like:
- `GET /management/v1/health/server?store=true` adds the declared window to `store`, from the settings, never read
  from the database, so it is there when the database does not answer too.
- The Store panel shows it as the window the deployment declares, with a plain line saying what relies on it (orphan
  removal waits past it; a dropped table is kept longer than it) and that the database's own backups must actually
  keep that long. The "is not shown" wording is gone from the panel, the health page doc and item 139.
- A fast route test for the field; `ConsoleHealthBrowserTest` (heavy) checks the row against the settings.
