Effort: L, a new Gradle module (the engine port and its golden, the world's calendar, securities, prices, accounts and personas, the live publisher) and a new leg of `demo.sh up` (Kafka Connect in the base stack, the sink, the change feed, the generator's Deployment); waits on nothing

The demo's data is a handful of sample tables (`demo/LakeWriter.java`): Xetra trades, FX rates, US quotes, and a toy
`finance.ledger` with a `journal` and an `accounts` table. Nothing joins, nothing ties out, and none of it is a
brokerage. D48 and `docs/design/brokerage-world.md` set out the generator; this is its first phase.

Paul, while it was under way (2026-11-22, D48 amended): "The demo should be real-time demo that is generating data like
a real-time eventing system would be. Data should be coming in through both kafka and singlestore." So the first plan's
Java writer, a Job committing a backfilled month, became a live event source.

What to build:
- `demo-datagen`, a Gradle module: the demo kit, forked once from Paul's ledger kit v14 (D48 as amended: the ledger
  kit serves the sequencer, this one only fills Iceberg), with its posting engine ported to Java, reading the 22 booking
  schemes and the chart of accounts as data exported from the kit (`defs.py`, `schemes_v20.py`, checked against the
  estate guard's whole deny list first; nothing else from the kit enters the repo).
- The world at its simplest: the NYSE calendar with T+1, about 300 synthetic securities with identifiers that cannot be
  real (the design's list), five-minute bars and closing prices, accounts and personas that produce only the
  transactions the kit has templates for (30 to 35, 40 to 47, 51 and 52, 60 to 65). Seed, anchor date and scale give
  the same events.
- The generator, live: `bootstrap` (a Job in `demo.sh up`) makes the topics, the `brokerage` namespaces, the tables of
  `brokerage.reference`, `brokerage.markets`, `brokerage.party.accounts` and `brokerage.ledger`, and the views
  `buying_power` and `running_collected_balance` (R17: derived, never posted); `run` (a Deployment) plays the world on
  a compressed clock, a business day in 10 minutes after a catch-up of three days. Reference data, market data and
  the ledger's output go to Kafka, and one Iceberg sink connector routes each record to its table; orders go to
  SingleStore `brokerage.orders`, and its change feed brings them in as `brokerage.trading.orders`. Each day ends with
  a tag `eod-YYYY-MM-DD` on every table that changed, once the sink has committed the day. `financial_transactions`
  starts on the v13 shape and gains `locate_id` when short selling starts. A restart resumes where the last run stopped.
- Kafka Connect moves from the CDC leg into the base stack; the CDC leg pauses the world while it runs, to keep its
  memory budget. Classifications and the regulated mark as the design's namespace table gives them.
- The toy `finance.ledger` tables go (the D27 refusal they show moves to `party.beneficial_owners` in item 152; until
  then the console shows none).

What done looks like:
- In the fast build: the port reproduces `events_v8.json` (44 financial transactions, 289 journal lines, every one
  matching) and the kit's own checks (`kit_checks()` in `schemes_v20.py`: LotNotHeld, BalanceBelowZero, the mirror
  proof, the rounding cases); the same seed gives the same events twice; a 1,000-account month posts with no halt;
  every value the world sends fits a column of its table; a restart mid-day leaves every topic and the orders as an
  unbroken run would.
- On the demo: the console shows `brokerage.ledger` gaining a snapshot every commit interval and a tag per business
  day, `brokerage.trading.orders` changing as orders fill, and the views answer in Trino as `ana`.

Choices made (Paul may overrule): Java rather than Python; the root namespace `brokerage`; 1,000 accounts; a business
day in 10 minutes; three catch-up days.

## Done, 2026-11-23 (worktree-brokerage-world-1)

`demo-datagen` holds the demo kit (the chart and the 22 booking schemes as JSON, checked against the estate guard), its
posting engine in Java, the world (NYSE calendar with T+1, 300 synthetic securities, 1,000 accounts across four
personas, orders, fills, settlements, expiries, month-end margin interest) and the live side: `bootstrap` makes the
topics, namespaces, tables and both views and fixes the world's settings and start offsets in `brokerage`'s
properties; `run` plays the world on a compressed clock to Kafka and SingleStore, tags each changed table
`eod-YYYY-MM-DD` once its snapshot holds the day's rows, adds `locate_id` before the first short sale, and resumes from
the topics' end offsets. The demo: Kafka Connect in the base stack, the Iceberg sink `brokerage-lake` (writing as
`cdc`, the worker's identity), SingleStore `brokerage.orders` and its change feed, the bootstrap Job, governance once
per lake and the generator's Deployment in every `up`; `up`, `rate` and `engines` stop Connect and the generator while
they run, `cdc` pauses the generator; the toy `finance.ledger` tables are gone. `status/2026-11-22-brokerage-world-1.md`
has the detail.

Checked in the fast build, 60 tests in `demo-datagen`: the port posts exactly what the kit's Python posted for
`events_v8.json` and matches the workbook per block; the kit's 46 checks; same seed, same events; a 1,000-account
month posts with no halt; every value fits its column and every column is filled; a mid-day restart with lost records
resumes to the same topics, orders and tags; against the in-memory catalog, the bootstrap, `locate_id` and day-end
tagging. The demo's scripts were checked statically only (`bash -n`, `kubectl apply --dry-run=client`).
