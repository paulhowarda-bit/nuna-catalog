## Done, 2026-11-23 (claude/console-wayfinding-d47d6a)

When every job type (`compaction`, `snapshot-expiry`, `orphan-removal`, `manifest-rewrite`) is off, Overview and
Maintenance lead with "Maintenance is off in lakehouse's policy: nothing will be compacted or expired.", linked to
the policy, and the old sentence, "17 tables not assessed yet: the maintenance worker has not reported.", is the second
line; Overview's In shape figure and its queue panel say the same (`queue.js` `maintenanceOff`, `catalogOff`). The
catalog's policy is read through one of the caller's own tables (`GET …/tables/<t>/maintenance-policy`, describe on
it): when that table's job keys each come from the catalog or the built-in policy, they are the catalog's. Reading the
catalog's own policy needs describe on the catalog, which a reader granted only a namespace lacks, and would write a
denial. Since the worker assesses only where compaction is on, the catalog's headline says "off" only while none of
the caller's tables is assessed; with a job on anywhere it leads as before. A table's Maintenance tab says the same
from its own resolved policy ("this table's policy" when a nearer level sets a key), and its Overview's Maintenance
panel too. Checked live in the demo as `ana` on `lakehouse` (built-in policy, every job off) through the swap: both
pages and `markets.fx.rates`'s tab. `ConsoleHealthTest#aTableNotAssessedYetIsNeitherInShapeNorQueued` (heavy): the
newcomer's headlines, figure, queue panel and table tab; bite `console-maintenance-off-unsaid`. Seen in passing, not
changed: the policy panels show a built-in key as "this built-in", since `health.js` `sourceWords` expects `default`
and the server answers `built-in`.
