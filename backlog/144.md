Paul's click-through of the demo (2026-11-22) found the console's Audit page, the catalog-wide feed of item 125
(`GET /management/v1/catalogs/{catalog}/audit/feed` and `/audit/summary`), answering 503 in the nuna-demo stack:
"The audit tables are not configured on this server (nuna.audit.tables.catalog)". The demo declares no audit
catalog (`demo/k8s/catalog-values.yaml`, `demo-values.yaml`) and runs no audit table writer (`catalog-audit-tables`,
D23, item 27), so the redesign's main audit view cannot be shown.

The feed needs `select` on the audit catalog (Paul, 2026-11-22: "Audit readers only"), and reads the table the writer
fills, so the demo needs:
- an audit catalog declared on the server, with `nuna.audit.tables.catalog`, `readers` and `writers`;
- the writer running, signed in as a principal of its own whose group holds nothing elsewhere;
- a readers' group for `gary` (who reads the audit trail) and `presenter`. It cannot be `demo-grant-admins`: the
  server refuses to start when the readers' group is a grant admin group (D18).

Done looks like: after `demo/demo.sh up`, the console's Audit page shows records for `gary` and `presenter`, and
refuses `ana`; demo/README.md says what is loaded, who reads the feed, and the writer's memory.
