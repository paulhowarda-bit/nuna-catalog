`build-plan.md`, M8 finding for decision: the ETag includes the credential epoch, a hash of the vended access key.
Each server mints and caches its own credentials, so a conditional load answered by another server is a 200: 0 of
32 in the SLO run. Behind the Kubernetes Service, which is not sticky, the 304 path (D14) mostly misses. The
deployment entry of 2026-11-11 says it needs deciding before production.

Two ways, for Paul: derive the epoch from a shared issuance window so servers agree, or accept sticky routing.

Done when Paul has chosen, and two servers answer one client's conditional loads with 304s (or the chart routes
stickily), with a test.

**Decided 2026-11-19 (D14):** a shared epoch ("Shared epoch"): servers derive the credential epoch from a shared issuance window, so they agree on the ETag; not sticky routing.

Done: the credential epoch is the issuance window a load falls in, so every server gives the same ETag.
- `StsCredentialVending` (catalog-server, `storage/`): the epoch hashes `floor(now / window)` on the server's clock,
  counted from the Unix epoch, with the profile, location, access, principal, subject and engine the credentials
  are for. The window is a quarter of `nuna.storage.credential-lifetime`, 7.5 minutes by default, so servers with
  the same lifetime share it; no new setting. Another caller or access level is still another ETag.
- Cached credentials are renewed with less than a window, the refresh margin and a minute left (13.5 of 30 minutes
  by default, was 7.5), so every set handed out in a window lasts through its end and the margin. A set is reused
  for 16.5 minutes instead of 22.5: about a third more AssumeRole calls per caller, table and server.
- Credentials that would not last through their window and the margin (a margin near the lifetime, as
  `VendingNearExpiryTest` sets it, or STS's clock far behind) keep the access key's hash as their epoch, as before,
  so no other server answers 304 for them and the route's margin check covers the one that holds them. A client
  thus never gets a 304 for credentials with less than the margin, less a server's clock lag, left; a server whose
  clock straddles a boundary costs it a 304, nothing worse. `TableRoutes` and `ETags` are unchanged.
- `SharedEpochTest` (catalog-server, `rest/`, Java only, 6 tests, a stand-in STS and movable clocks): two servers
  with their own keys give one ETag within a window and a new one after it; a client going round three servers
  every 20 s for 2 hours loads in full 16 times, once per window, and gets 304 on the other 344, never for
  credentials near expiry; every vend at 15, 30 and 60-minute lifetimes lasts through its window plus the margin
  and two servers agree; per-credential epochs where credentials cannot last. Three bites in `bites/D14/`, each run
  once here and failing on an assertion; M6's `renewed-inside-margin` recorded again against the moved line.
  `CredentialExpiryTest` needed no change: its stand-in vending makes its own epochs.
