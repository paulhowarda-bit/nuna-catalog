## What is missing

The console learns that a grant is refused only after the grant is sent: the self-grant refusal ("no one grants to a
group they belong to", `GrantRoutes.java:289`), the separation-of-duties refusals, and a privilege that means
nothing on its object (the server accepts `create` on a table). Atelier's grant dialog (item 248) shows, before
Grant is pressed, one line saying what the grant will do, that it is already held, or why it would be refused, with
Grant disabled (Atelier, 2026-11-24).

## Done when

- `POST /management/v1/catalogs/{catalog}/grants/preview` takes a grant's body and answers `admitted`, `unchanged`
  (already held, with who granted it and when) or `refused` with the reason the grant route would give, writing
  nothing. Like the existing `/check` route it is not audited.
- It refuses callers who may not manage grants on the object as the grant route does.
- Route tests cover each answer, and a bite shows the self-grant refusal is checked.
- A privilege that means nothing on its object (`create` on a table or view) is refused by both preview and grant.

## What was done

`POST /management/v1/catalogs/{catalog}/grants/preview` takes the grant's body and answers 200 with
`{"securable", "group", "privilege", "answer"}`: `admitted`; `unchanged`, with `granted-by` and `granted-at`; or
`refused`, with `refusal` (`privilege-not-on-object`, `self-grant` or `separation-of-duties`) and `reason`, the
grant route's own message. It writes nothing. The grant route and the preview share one path in `GrantRoutes`
(`asked`, `refusal`, `admits`), so they cannot drift apart; the separation check is the same code, asked by the grant
inside the store's write and by the preview over the group's grants it reads. A caller that may not manage grants
there gets the grant route's 403 or 404, audited as `previewGrant`; the answers are not audited.

`create` on a table or view is refused: 400 at the grant route (a mistake in the request, unaudited),
`privilege-not-on-object` at the preview. A revoke still removes one granted before.

catalog-security reviewed it: nothing blocks it. Acted on: `Separation.refusal` names the first conflict in
`Privilege`'s order, so preview and grant name the same privilege whatever order the store reads; the preview's
javadoc records the duty map it gives a scoped grant admin without a record; the missing bites. `write` on a view,
which means nothing there either, is backlog 258.

Tests: `GrantPreviewTest` (new, 11), preview cases in `GrantHiddenNamesTest`, an order case in `SeparationTest`; the
route is listed in the heavy `MutatingRoutesAuditTest`'s routes that change nothing. Bites: six new in `bites/D18/`,
three recorded again; all but `M5/self-grant-allowed` (heavy) run and bite. Documented in `build-plan.md`, M7, "Who
may call the management routes"; `status/2026-11-24-grant-preview-246.md`.
