The export dialog says "The export is audited." (`catalog-server/src/main/resources/nuna/ui/governance.js:270`),
but `GrantRoutes.export` and `RowColumnReadRoutes` write no audit record when grants or rules are exported. The
console promises what the server does not do.

What done looks like: catalog-security decides whether an export must be audited. If so, each export writes a
record (who, which catalog, how many grants) with a test; if not, the console's sentence goes.

## What was done

catalog-security's answer: an export is audited. A new record type, `nuna.catalog.export` (`Export`: the
catalog, what was exported, `grants` or `row-column-rules`, how many, and who), published synchronously before the
answer as a read is (D23), by `GrantRoutes.export` and `RowColumnReadRoutes`' export. The codec's pinned file, the
audit rows and the sample events carry it; the console's audit panel words it ("exported 12 grants") among the reads.
`RowColumnReadRoutesTest` checks both rule exports' records; `SecurityGateTest` checks the grants export's (heavy,
left to the overnight run). Bite `bites/D23/export-unaudited.patch` bites.
