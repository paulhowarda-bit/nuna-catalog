## What is wrong

Paul, 2026-11-24: "why does the screen come up into audit instead of overview." He signs in to the demo as
`presenter`, whom Keycloak puts in all five groups (demo/README.md). `homeKey` sends auditors and grant admins to Audit
before anything else (Atelier, 2026-11-23), so presenter opened on Audit, with Audit first in the rail.

## Decision

Atelier, 2026-11-24, option (c): only a caller who holds every duty (audit open on the catalog, operates, and reads
data) lands on Overview; everyone else keeps the order of 2026-11-23. D18 gives each group one duty plus reading, so a
real auditor or operator who also reads data stays on the page where their work is; only a showcase sign-in holds
both the audit and the operator duty, and a demo should open on the product's front page and walk the rail in reading
order.

## Done when

`homeKey` returns `overview` when `auditOpen(c) && operates() && readsData()`, before the other checks, so the rail
keeps its usual order for that caller. `ConsoleBrowserTest#eachRoleSeesWhatItsGroupsCanUseAndLandsWhereItsWorkIs`
signs in as a caller in the test group, the grant admins and the platform operators at once (`Api#everyDuty`) and
expects `#p=overview&c=trading` and the rail overview, catalog, refs, maintenance, governance, audit, service.

## What was done

`homeKey` (app.js) checks `auditOpen(c) && operates() && readsData()` first and returns `overview`; the rail follows
it, so that caller's rail keeps its usual order. `Api#everyDuty` signs in the test group, the grant admins and the
platform operators at once, and `ConsoleBrowserTest#eachRoleSeesWhatItsGroupsCanUseAndLandsWhereItsWorkIs` expects it
on the overview with the rail in that order. The browser test is heavy and refuses beside the demo: the overnight run
runs it.
