# 267. demo.sh's Kafka Connect calls retry a 502, 503, 504 or 409 instead of stopping up

Status: done
Done: 2026-11-25 on worktree-brokerage-routes
Added: 2026-11-24 by local_0484717c-9adc-4b18-af93-2db236201f4a
Touches: demo/demo.sh
Effort: S, one function in demo.sh, checked against a stub server that answers 502 and 409 before 200

Paul, 2026-11-24, told that `up` can stop on one 502 from Kafka Connect: "Yes, fix it".

**What is wrong.** `connect_api` in `demo/demo.sh` fails on any answer but 2xx. Under it, `tls_curl` tries again
only when the connection is refused or the TLS handshake fails (curl's 7 and 35), so a 502, 503 or 504 from the
front door, or Connect's own 409 while its workers rebalance, stops the script at once, though `connect_api` keeps a
PUT's body in a file "so a second try sends it again".

**The evidence.** On 2026-11-24 Catalog UI Expert's `demo/demo.sh up` (main at 570db200) finished the core stack and
stopped at the brokerage world's step: Kafka Connect answered 502 to `PUT /connectors/brokerage-lake/config`.
Connect had passed its readiness probe and `wait_for_connect`; Windows had 765 MB free and was paging hard. Its pod
showed no restarts. `demo/demo.sh gen` run again finished.

**What done looks like.** `connect_api` tries a call again, every 2 s for about a minute, while Connect or the front
door answers 409, 502, 503 or 504, and fails as before on anything else or when the minute runs out, naming the last
answer. A stub server that answers 502 and then 409 before 200 sees the call succeed; one that answers 502 for good
sees it fail after the minute, and one that answers 404 sees it fail at once.

**What was done** (2026-11-25). `connect_api` sends a call up to 30 times, 2 s apart, while the answer is 409, 502,
503 or 504, and fails as before on any other answer or after the last try, naming it; a PUT sends the same body each
time. A refused connection after `tls_curl`'s own three tries now fails with the message (`answered :`) instead of
stopping the script without one. Checked with `demo.sh`'s own `tls_curl` and `connect_api` against a stub server:
502, 409 and 503 then 200 succeeds after 4 requests with the same body and 3 sleeps; 502 for good fails after 30
requests and 29 sleeps (58 s); 404 fails at once with 1 request; 504 then 200 succeeds; and a failing call still stops
a `set -euo pipefail` caller. Not run against the demo: the 502 came from a stall that cannot be made on purpose.
