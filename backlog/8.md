# 8. findCommit waits within the retry budget when the store is down

`build-plan.md`, M8 follow-ups: `findCommit` answers "unknown" at once when the store is down, where it could wait
within the retry budget (P10: at most 4 attempts or 2 s) and often settle the outcome.

Done when a commit whose outcome is unknown during a short outage is settled within the budget, shown by a fault
test, and an outage longer than the budget still answers as today.

Done: when `findCommit` throws `StoreUnavailableException` (the store cannot be reached), the commit engine asks it
again within the engine's own P10 bounds, `CommitSettings.maxAttempts` (4) and `retryBudget` (2 s), with the commit
loop's backoff (100, 200, 400 ms, doubling, up to 10% jitter): at most 4 asks per unknown outcome, and none that
would start after 2 s from the request's start, so the whole request stays inside one budget. When the store
answers, the outcome is settled as before: COMMITTED is a success, NOT_COMMITTED lets the loop send the commit again
if the budget allows. An outage that outlasts the budget answers exactly as before, 500 CommitStateUnknownException
with the same message; any other `StoreException` from `findCommit` (the store answers but cannot decide) still
answers at once. One method changed, `CommitEngine.settle`, which every path settling through `findCommit` shares:
commit, create, register (a new object or a revive) and the multi-table transaction.

Tests, in `catalog-core`'s `CommitEngineTest` against the existing store double (`FaultyStore` over the shared
PostgreSQL): a short outage (two failed asks) settles a landed commit; a 600 ms outage on the test clock settles a
commit that did not land, which is then sent again and lands once; an outage longer than the budget answers the same
500 after 4 asks; with 10 attempts allowed no ask starts after 2 s (5 asks); a COMMIT that hung for 1.95 s leaves
no time to ask again; a create settles across a short outage; a store that answers but cannot decide is asked once.
`CommitEngineTest$Idempotency#outageWhileSettlingStaysUnknown` now uses an outage that outlasts the budget. Bite
recorded: `bites/M8/findcommit-answers-at-once.patch`; `bites/M8/unknown-commit-answers-503.patch` recorded again,
the same break, against the new code.
