## What was done

The world (demo-datagen) now keeps the firm's books beside the ledger, the month-end close is one commit with a tag,
a trade busted after the close is restated on a branch and merged, and every tie of the design is a view in
`brokerage.recon`.

- Margin (`world/Margin.java`, `brokerage.margin`): each security's house requirement daily; each margin account at
  the close with Reg T initial, FINRA 4210(c) maintenance and the house requirement, the binding one, the money
  balance, the house surplus and the intraday margin deficit of FINRA 4210(f)(8) as amended by Notice 26-10 (the
  end-of-day method); calls: intraday (met when the surplus rises by its amount, expired after 15 business
  days) and maintenance (due in two business days, then liquidation due).
- Tax (`world/Tax.java`, `brokerage.tax`): the open tax lots at each close, each lot's part of a sale, wash sales
  (IRC 1091: a loss washed by a buy 30 days either side, the loss moved into the replacement lot's basis), and at the
  close the 1099-B and 1099-DIV lines.
- Reporting (`world/Reporting.java`): a confirm per order and trade date (10b-10); CAT events, MENO, MEOR and the
  venue's trade, for every acceptance, route and fill; at the close the Rule 606 report and best-execution reviews.
- Regulatory (`world/Regulatory.java`, `brokerage.regulatory`): the 15c3-3 reserve computation weekly, as of the
  week's last business day; net capital at the close (minimum the greater of $250,000 and 2% of aggregate debit items).
- Asset servicing (`brokerage.reference.corporate_actions`, `brokerage.asset_servicing`): each corporate action the
  kit books (item 155), entitlements at the ex-date and income payments.
- The month-end close (`world/MonthEnd.java`, `live/MonthEndWriter.java`): built at the 17:00 end of a month's last
  business day, written as one `/transactions/commit` across statements, statement positions, 1099-B, 1099-DIV, the
  Rule 606 report, net capital and best-execution reviews, then tagged `month-end-YYYY-MM` through the catalog's refs.
  Idempotent: a close whose tag exists, or whose commit is already in main's history, is not written again.
- The seeded bust: at 07:15 on the first business day after a close, the last buy fill of the month's last day whose
  lot is untouched and due to settle that day is busted (template 92, new in the kit's booking schemes; the order
  management system's execution `trade_busted`, a CNS adjustment `-B`). The restated statements go on branch
  `restate-YYYY-MM`, written there and merged; the tag keeps what was first published.
- The General Ledger (`live/GeneralLedger.java`): one control account per customer money definition (SL-2, 1000 to
  1999), `GL-1001` being Customer Cash Balances [Control]; Oracle `treasury.gl_journal` (each day's debits and
  credits per control) and `treasury.gl_balances` (each control at each day's end, on its normal side), loaded by
  `Main cdc` from the books (`Sources.Book`) beside the bank's payments, captured by Debezium into
  `history.oracle.treasury`, and read through views in `brokerage.finance` (`gl_journal`, `gl_balances`, and
  `bank_payments` without the counterparty's bank details), made once the histories exist.
- Ties (`live/Recon.java`): 2 `tax_lots_vs_ledger`, 5 `payments_vs_ledger` and `bank_vs_ledger`, 6
  `gl_vs_subledger`, 7 `statements_vs_ledger`, 8 `realized_vs_1099b`, 9 `cat_vs_orders`; tie 4 takes a bust off its
  buy. Each is empty on a clean world (ReconTest, in H2, on two weeks and on a world crossing September's end); on
  the seeded one, `cns_vs_fills` shows the fail alone, and `statements_vs_ledger` on main shows the restated
  account's two lines, unsettled purchases and the busted position, and nothing at the tag.
- The demo: 12 new feeds in the world's sink (`demo/brokerage/connector.json`) and SingleStore's tables; Oracle's
  two new tables in `demo/cdc/oracle.sql`, its connectors (`oracle-source.json`, `oracle-history.json`, now routing
  by `_cdc.source`, with every column renamed to lower case), `demo.sh`'s `TREASURY_TABLES` and `HISTORY_TABLES`; a
  CDC leg begun before 153 starts anew once (`cdc-begun` world=153), as Oracle's init script makes the tables only
  on an empty volume; `brokerage_books_governance` classifies the new namespaces and owns them.
- Bites: 10 in `bites/WORLD-153/`, every one BITES.

Choices made here, Paul may overrule:

- The house margin rates: 30% long at $10 or more, 35% from $5, 50% from $3, 100% below; 35% short, 100% under $5;
  100% on hard-to-borrow; 50% concentration over 70% of a long value over $25,000. The world's accounts are rarely
  levered enough to be called, so `MarginTest` checks the calls directly and a month of the world may show none.
- The GL is summarized from the sub-ledger, one entry per control a day, rather than a chart of the firm's own
  (bank, revenue, capital accounts), which the world does not keep; tie 6 checks every control, not only GL-1001.
- The close is written by the generator's Java writer, not Spark: Spark needs Trino stopped, and the writer needs no
  more memory than the generator has.
- `brokerage.finance` has no `bank_accounts` (the world has two settlement accounts, named in `bank_payments`), and
  its `bank_payments` leaves out the counterparty's routing and account numbers and name, so no flag is needed.
- Tie 5's bank side holds what the last `cdc` loaded: `bank_vs_ledger` compares only the days up to the General
  Ledger's last day, and a deposit returned is left out of both sides (the bank's table keeps each payment's latest
  status alone).
- Net capital and the reserve stay in the lake (`brokerage.regulatory`), not Oracle.
- Governance: `margin`, `tax`, `client_reporting`, `regulatory` confidential, `asset_servicing` internal, `finance`
  confidential and regulated, each with an owner.

Only the live demo can check (none of it was run against the stack):

- `demo/demo.sh gen` at a month end: the close as one commit (the catalog's audit shows one `/transactions/commit`),
  tag `month-end-YYYY-MM` in the console's refs; the next morning branch `restate-YYYY-MM` made and merged;
  `SELECT * FROM lakehouse."brokerage.recon".statements_vs_ledger` gives the restated account's two lines, and
  reading `statements` through warehouse `lakehouse@month-end-YYYY-MM` gives the payable as first published.
- `demo/demo.sh cdc` on a leg begun before 153: it says it starts anew once; then the three treasury histories fill
  (`wait_for_rows`), with lower-case columns; `brokerage.finance`'s three views appear at the world's next end of
  day; `gl_vs_subledger` and `bank_vs_ledger` empty, `payments_vs_ledger` empty. Oracle's DATE lands as a timestamp
  and NUMBER(20, 2) as `decimal(38, 2)`; the views cast them back, which only Trino runs.
- The finance views order each key's changes by `_cdc.ts`, then `_cdc.offset`, and drop a key whose newest change
  is a delete: that the sink's `_cdc` fields have those names and values (`I`, `U`, `D`) as the README says.
- The other ties empty on the demo's world, both routes' tables read, and Trino's dialect of each view (H2 ran them).
- The governance and `regulated` on `brokerage.finance`: that the generator, a trusted engine, still makes the
  finance views there.
