# 129. Grants: the effective grants on an object, inherited ones included (D45)

Effort: M, a read that resolves a securable's grants through its namespaces and catalog, with tests

Each table's Grants tab in the prototype lists who may do what on it, inherited grants included, and says where
each grant comes from (D45). Today `GET /grants/on` lists only one securable's own grants, without those of its
namespaces and catalog.

What done looks like:
- A read of the effective grants on a catalog, namespace, table or view, each with the securable it was granted on.
- Tests that a grant on a parent namespace or on the catalog appears, and that a revoke removes it.

Who may read it (settled on the recommendation, 2026-11-22): whoever holds `manage-grants` on the securable or
above it, as for `GET /grants/on`, the read by object, with a refusal answered as there (403 and a `Denial`, or the
404 of a missing name to a caller that holds nothing on it, D18 as amended for item 73). The grants on the namespaces
and catalog above an object decide who may use it, so they belong to what its grant admin reviews, and the
prototype's Grants tab shows them to such a caller. Holding `manage-grants` on the catalog was the other reading;
it would close the tab to a namespace's own grant admins.

What was done (2026-11-22):
- `Authorizer.effectiveGrants(Target)` lists every grant that reaches a securable, from its catalog down through each
  namespace above it to the securable itself, along the chain `decide` walks, leaving out privileges this version
  does not know as `decide` does.
- `GET /management/v1/catalogs/{catalog}/grants/effective?type=&namespace=&name=` answers
  `{"securable", "grants": [{"securable", "group", "privilege", "granted-by", "granted-at"}], "grant-admin-groups"}`,
  not paged, documented in `GrantRoutes`. It landed on main in c077676.
- Tests: `EffectiveGrantsTest` (catalog-security, 8 tests, fast build), including agreement with `decide` for every
  group, securable and privilege of a small tree; `SecurityGateTest#theEffectiveGrantsOnATableIncludeEveryGrantAboveIt`
  (heavy, not run here): a grant on a namespace above a table appears, a revoke removes it, the steward may read,
  the analyst is refused and on hr answered as for a missing table.
- Bite: `bites/D45/effective-grants-own-only.patch` (read only the target's own grants); 6 of the 8 unit tests failed
  under it, and it applies (`git apply --check`).
