## What is missing

Nothing tells a catalog's owner which of the four steps the maintenance worker needs is missing (item 250 has the
evidence). The console's checklist (item 252) needs one answer per step (Atelier, 2026-11-24).

## Done when

`GET /management/v1/catalogs/{catalog}/maintenance/readiness`, for those who may `describe` the catalog, answers four
steps, each `done`, `missing`, `refused` or `unknown`, and the time of the worker's last report:

- `grant`: the worker's last-reported groups hold `write` on the catalog.
- `storage`: each allowed prefix of the catalog's storage profile lies beneath a prefix the worker reported, matched
  the way `Storage.io()` matches. (If item 256 has landed: the worker holds an identity for the profile's store.)
- `store`: the worker's last list check there succeeded and no delete there was refused.
- `policy`: the catalog's resolved policy turns at least one job on.

Before any worker has reported, the first three are `unknown`. Names (the worker's group, prefixes, profiles) go only to
those who may modify the catalog or manage its grants, and to platform operators. Route tests cover each state, and a
bite shows an uncovered prefix is reported `missing`.

## Done (2026-11-24)

- `GET /management/v1/catalogs/{catalog}/maintenance/readiness` (`ReadinessRoutes`): `catalog`, `names`,
  `reported-at` (the last storage report's end, or its start while it runs; absent before one), then `grant`,
  `storage`, `store` and `policy`, each with `state`. Shape and rules: docs/observability/table-health.md, "Readiness".
  - `grant`: a group of the newest storage scan's token holds a grant on the catalog itself giving `write` (`write` or
    `modify`), one `grantsFor` read; a grant on a namespace does not count. With names, `groups`:
    `[{"group", "write"}]`.
  - `storage`: every allowed prefix of the catalog's profile is covered by a reported prefix
    (`MaintenanceScan.covering`, the worker's matching). With names, `profile`, `endpoint` (not for AWS S3) and
    `prefixes: [{"prefix", "covered-by": {"kind", "endpoint", "prefix"}}]`.
  - `store`: listing from the newest scan with storage, deletes from the newest such scan that has ended (a scan
    under way has reported no deletes, so it does not hide the last one's refusals). `refused` with `code` for a
    refused listing or delete; `unknown` for an uncovered prefix, or with `code` for a listing that failed; a refusal
    outweighs an unknown. With names, per prefix what the store answered.
  - `policy`: the catalog's resolved policy (`GovernanceRoutes.resolvePolicy`); `jobs` lists the jobs on, for everyone.
- Needs `describe` on the catalog (`guard.require`), refusals audited, reads not, as the governance summary and policy
  reads. Names for `modify` or `manage-grants` on the catalog (`guard.allows`) and platform operators
  (`ManagementGuard.operator`, now package-visible).
