What was done: the console decides from the caller's own grants before it asks, and the tabs stay, as Atelier's
amendment asks.
- A table or view (table.js) is loaded only when a grant of the caller's groups that reaches it holds select, write or
  modify (`readsHere`, requests.js, from the same own-grants answer as `heldHere`). Otherwise Overview, Schema, History
  and Maintenance, and a view's Overview and Versions, show a closed note without asking: "Reading this table's data
  needs select on it, which your groups do not hold. Its grants and governance are on Access." (`unreadWords`,
  access.js). The health call for the header's request pills is skipped too, and its governance marks are asked only of
  a caller who may describe it (`describesHere`: any grant reaching it, or managing the catalog's grants). When the own
  grants cannot be read, the load is asked as before and the catalog decides.
- The rail (app.js `setChrome`) draws Catalog and Branches and tags only for a caller who holds something in the catalog
  (`holdsIn`, access.js: the catalog list names a catalog only to such a caller, except to a platform operator, for whom
  one of its `may` privileges must be true). A link to them, for an operator who holds nothing, says so without asking.
- His Governance (governance.js, outside the Touches line, since the page is there) is the DR freeze alone: a "Freeze a
  namespace for DR" page with no summary and no holds list, and an empty `#operator-freezes` for item 323's list of the
  freezes he may lift. Item 323 is not built.
- The format badge stays from the load: the catalog's listing carries no format, so a caller who does not read the
  table sees none. A view's "view" badge is now drawn without its load.
- Tests (ConsoleBrowserTest): `browsingWritesNoRefusalTheCallersOwnGrantsForetell` (new) walks gary's, cora's and
  oscar's pages and reads the audit log (`AuditLogCapture`): no denial for any of them but one asked for on purpose at
  the end. `eachRoleSeesWhatItsGroupsCanUseAndLandsWhereItsWorkIs` expects the operator's rail as Service and Governance
  and his freeze page; `grantsHaveARailItemAndAGrantAdminOpensATableOrViewOnAccess` expects the closed note on gary's
  Overview; `refusalsAreShownExactlyAsTheCatalogAnswered` expects it too, and checks the catalog's refusal verbatim in
  the Export metadata dialog, which the grant admin asks for himself. All are heavy (Selenium) and were not run: the
  demo holds the Podman machine. `ConsoleRulesTest` and `ConsoleRefWordsTest` pass; `:bitesApply` passes.
- Bites (recorded, not run): `UI/console-unread-table-loaded`, `UI/console-rail-catalog-for-operator` and
  `UI/console-operator-governance-asks-summary` (new); `UI/console-closed-table-reads-as-flagged`,
  `UI/console-rail-no-grants` and `UI/console-rail-no-refs` recorded again with the same breaks, since this moved their
