A month at a brokerage has events v14 cannot book, and the generator produces none until the demo kit can:

- Money and fees (for item 152): wire deposit received; wire withdrawal initiated, settled and cancelled; an ACH
  deposit returned before and after settlement, with its Nacha return code; a cash journal between two brokerage
  accounts; an account fee charged and reversed (wire, ACATS-out, hard-to-borrow on shorts); short sell and buy to
  cover cancelled.
- Transfers and asset servicing (for items 152 and 153): ACATS received (cash, and positions whose lots carry the
  delivering firm's cost and acquisition date) and delivered (lots out at remaining cost, no gain); a cash dividend on
  a long position; a payment in lieu charged to a short position; forward and reverse splits (lots rescaled, cost
  unchanged, cash in lieu of fractions).

The bar: every template books on all four sub-ledgers with the engine's self-checks holding after every Posting
(each journal entry balanced in its unit, lots tied to their controls, mirrors, J16 floors), and reads as believable to
a brokerage person.

What done looks like: for each template, a lifecycle on a cash and a margin account runs through the engine in the
fast build with every self-check passing, and the generator produces those events.

## What was done

**Calculations.** OpenLotsReceived (kind 11) opens a lot per received element whose reference is the delivering
firm's cost and the shares received, so its remaining cost (R12) carries over. A received lot's id is the receipt's
id times 1,000 plus its place in it (1 to 999). RescaleLots and RescaleShortLots (12 and 13) multiply each lot's free
shares and its reference shares by ratioNew / ratioOld and leave the reference cost or proceeds alone, so 1002 and
1107 do not move. The engine registers a calculation's lots after the lines post and before the self-checks. It
refuses a split that leaves a lot more decimals than the units hold (a new reason, FractionalShares), a ratio of zero
or less, and a received lot of no shares or a negative cost.

**Chart.** 1005 Dividends Receivable, 1108 Payment in Lieu Payable (mirrored by 5107 and 4109), 1203 Securities
Transferred at Cost (MayReverse), 1204 Dividend Income, 1304 Account Fees, 1305 Payment in Lieu of Dividends, 4109
and 5107. The retired and gap numbers (1104, 1106, 4106, 5105) stay unused.

**The world** draws the new events from a second random source, so the first draws as before. World 1 stays
deterministic for a seed and differs from before only where a new event changed a balance it reads. The fast build's
month (seed 20261001, 1,000 accounts, 22 business days) has 84 wire deposits, 55 wire withdrawals (51 sent, 4
cancelled), 17 ACH returns of 503 ACH deposits (8 before settlement, 9 after), 17 journals, 84 fees and 4 reversals,
30 short and 23 cover orders cancelled part-filled at the close, 6 ACATS cash and 28 position receipts, 7 position
and 1 cash deliveries, 77 split postings and 17 cash in lieu, 330 dividend entitlements and 37 payments, and 15
payments in lieu accrued and 1 paid. The rates:

- Wires in: 0.4% of active accounts a day, 0.1% of dormant ones. Wires out: 0.3% and 0.08%, a $25 fee on each sent,
  8% cancelled before release.
- One ACH deposit in forty returned: three in five before settlement (R01, R02, R03, R16), the rest one to three
  business days after (R01, R10, R08).
- Journals: 0.15% of active accounts a day, 0.03% of dormant ones.
- ACATS in: 0.06% of accounts a day, six in ten full; cash with seven in ten. ACATS out: 0.025% a day, a $75 fee, six
  in ten in full, which closes the account.
- One wire or ACATS fee in eight given back, one to three business days later.
- One short or cover order in ten part-filled and cancelled at the close.
- Every ETF and about 55% of stocks pay quarterly, each announcing on one business day in 63; ex-date five to eight
  business days on, paid five to fifteen after that.
- Each month a forward split (2:1, 3:1, 4:1, 3:2) of a dear popular stock and a reverse split (1:4 to 1:20) of a
  cheap one.
- Hard-to-borrow fees accrued daily on each short in one security in nine (15% to 45% a year), charged at month end.

**Choices made here, Paul may overrule:**

- 22 templates rather than about sixteen: a journal books on both accounts, and the dividend and the payment in lieu
  each have an entitlement and a payment.
- Both lists land together as one extension of the demo kit, not as a v15 and a v16.
- "Books on all four sub-ledgers" is read as: each template writes lines in every sub-ledger the event changes and no
  zero lines elsewhere (86 writes SL-SHARE only, 83 and 84 SL-CA and SL-SHARE, 88 SL-CA only), and all four
  sub-ledgers' self-checks hold after every posting.
- ACATS positions move at cost against an equity account of their own, 1203, not Net Deposits, so Net Deposits stays
  cash in less cash out. ACATS cash goes through Net Deposits like any deposit or withdrawal.
- A dividend is receivable at the ex-date on SL-CA only and reaches the clocks on the pay date: cash not yet received
  buys nothing. A payment in lieu is owed on both clocks at the ex-date, like accrued margin interest.
- One fee template with a feeType (wire_out, acats_out, hard_to_borrow) and one expense account.
- One split template for forward and reverse. Shares pending delivery or cover at the split are delivered in the
  shares they traded in. Cash in lieu is paid with the split, at the adjusted price, for the position's fraction
  only, so a lot may keep a fraction of a share; the world's sales name fractional lots.
- A received lot's acquisition date rides on the message (received_lots.acquired_date), and the world's FIFO orders
  lots by it; the kit keeps no date on a lot.
- Left out: withholding on dividends, interest on credit balances, ACATS out of an account with a debit or a short (the
  receiving firm taking them over). The world never splits a security anyone is short, nor shorts one with a split
  announced.
- Hard to borrow is private to the world for now; item 151's easy-to-borrow list may replace it.

**What items 152 and 153 can rely on.** Every new event goes out on both routes as a financial_transactions row and
its journal_lines rows, like any other. The new financial_transactions columns come after `accrued_through_date` (and
before `locate_id` in SingleStore): return_code, journal_id, contra_brokerage_account (long), fee_type,
acats_control_number, reason_code, contra_firm_number, transfer_type, received_lots (a list of lot, shares, cost,
acquired_date), corporate_action_id, ratio_new and ratio_old (long), ex_date, cash_in_lieu_price, record_date and
pay_date. The existing amount, payment_id, rate, period_start, period_end, released_amount and lots columns mean the
same on the new templates. security_id and quantity are set on 78 for hard to borrow and on 83 to 91. Identifiers:
payments `PAY-5…`, journals `JNL-8…`, ACATS control numbers `AC<yyyyMMdd><nnnn>`, contra firms like `S0412` (not a
DTC number), corporate actions `CA-6…`.
