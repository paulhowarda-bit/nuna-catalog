engine matrix) gets 403 on them.

## What was done

The brokerage world's writers are trusted engines and the ten tables that hold tax ids, birth dates, addresses or bank
account numbers are flagged, all in `demo/demo.sh`. Recorded in `status/2026-11-27-backlog-308.md`.

- **Trusted engines**: `TRUSTED_CLIENTS` (`trino-demo kafka-connect-demo singlestore-demo brokerage-world-demo`), each
  registered by `oscar` on every `up`, where `trino-demo` alone was. `kafka-connect-demo` is user `cdc`: the world's
  sinks `brokerage-lake` and `brokerage-lake-owners`, and the CDC leg's. `singlestore-demo` is user `singlestore`:
  SingleStore's change feeds. `brokerage-world-demo` is the generator's, which the item did not name: it loads every
  table it counts (`IcebergLake.records`, on a restart with a route off and in `drain`) and tags each end of day (a
  commit), so untrusted it would count a flagged table as 0 rows, re-send it and fail `drain`.
- **Flagged** (`FLAGGED_TABLES`, attribute `row-column-policy` set by `stella`): `brokerage.party.parties` and
  `beneficial_owners` (both hold tax id, birth date and address), `brokerage.money_movement.payments` (bank account
  number); their copies under `brokerage.singlestore`; and the CDC leg's `history.postgres.party.parties`,
  `history.postgres.party.beneficial_owners`, `history.postgres.money_movement.payments` and
  `history.oracle.treasury.bank_payments` (the counterparty's account number). The Kafka route's at every `gen`
  (`brokerage_backoffice_governance`), the SingleStore copies once the change feed has made them all
  (`world_feeds_start`), the histories at every `cdc` once its sinks have made them (`history_flags`). The
  current-state views read only these tables, so they are covered through them; a view has no files to flag. No row
  filter or column mask is set on them.
- **Test**: `demo-datagen`'s `FlaggedTablesTest` holds `FLAGGED_TABLES` to every lake table whose columns include
  `tax_id`, `birth_date`, `address_line`, `bank_account_number` or `counterparty_account_number` (both routes, from
  `LakeSchemas`; the histories, from `Sources`), and `TRUSTED_CLIENTS` to the clients Mint.java gives users `trino`,
  `cdc`, `singlestore` and `brokerage`. Bites `bites/WORLD-308/bank-payments-history-unflagged` and
  `change-feed-untrusted`.
- **Docs**: `demo/README.md` (the users table, what `up` makes, the brokerage world's governance) and
  `docs/design/brokerage-world.md` (the namespace tree and what the catalog gets to show).
