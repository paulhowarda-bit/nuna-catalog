whose `<datalist>` offers tonight's queue only. In the demo the queue is empty, so the field offers nothing, and a
datalist shows its choices only once typed into or double-clicked.

Done when the dialog is a dropdown that shows its choices without typing: every table in the catalog the caller's
groups may compact (a `modify` grant that reaches it), tonight's queue first, from what the console already reads (the
estate the page loads and the caller's own grants, no new route); typing a path still works for a table not listed.
A browser test checks a table that is not queued is offered and opens its compaction dialog, and that one the caller
may not modify is not offered; the bite is recorded. Checked live on the demo as a user who may modify and one who
may not (no button).
