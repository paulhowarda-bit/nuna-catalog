Effort: M, four refusals in two modules; the two commit-engine ones come back as a reply, not a throw, so the server needs a way to tell them apart from other 403s before it can audit them

Item 132 made a drop, rename, undrop or purge claim refused by a DR freeze publish a `nuna.catalog.denial` record, as
every refusal must (D18). Working on it found four more refusals that answer 403 and publish nothing:

- A commit refused by a DR freeze: `Rules.java`, line 92, "Writes to ... are frozen for DR reconcile: ...". It returns
  a `Rejection.forbidden`, which reaches the client as a `CommitReply.Failure` (`ObjectRoutes.reply`). The same rule
  set refuses a snapshot removal under a legal hold (`Rules.java`, line 83) the same way, also unaudited.
- An unregister refused by a DR freeze: `CommitEngine.java`, line 693, "... is frozen for DR reconcile and cannot be
  unregistered". Its legal-hold refusal beside it (line 689) is unaudited too. `TableRoutes.unregister` audits the
  regulated and flagged-table refusals through `Guard.refuse`, but not these.
- A drop refused by a legal hold: `ObjectRoutes.java`, line 157, `Http.forbidden(... "is under a legal hold and cannot
  be dropped")`, a `CatalogError` with no denial.
- A purge claim refused by a legal hold: `LifecycleRoutes.java`, line 219, `CatalogError.of(403, ...)` with no denial.

Evidence: read from the code on 2026-11-22 (branch of item 132). No test checks for a denial on any of them.

What done looks like:
- Each refusal above publishes a `nuna.catalog.denial` record through `Denials`, naming the operation and the table
  or view, with reason `dr_freeze` or `legal_hold` and the message the caller gets. The answer stays the same 403.
- A fast test per refusal, or per route, that checks the denial, in the form of `FrozenObjectRefusalTest` and
  `FrozenLifecycleRefusalTest` (item 132).
- A bite per publish site, in `bites/D18/`, that drops the publish and fails its test.

What was done (2026-11-22):
- Each refusal above publishes a `nuna.catalog.denial` record through `Denials`, naming the operation and the table,
  with reason `dr_freeze: ` or `legal_hold: ` and then the message the caller gets. The answer is unchanged: 403
  ForbiddenException, same message.
  - Commit and snapshot removal: `Rules.drFreeze` and `Rules.retentionHold` reject with `Rejection.refused(reason,
    message)`, which carries the reason (`CommitRule.Rejection.denial`). The engine passes it on as
    `CommitReply.Failure.refusal`: the object refused and the reason. That is how the server tells these 403s from
    the others.
  - Unregister: the engine answers the store's `HELD` and `FROZEN` refusals with `Failure.refused`, the same way.
  - `Guard.refused` publishes the denial for a reply that carries a refusal. Every route that runs the commit engine
    calls it before rendering the reply: table create, commit, register and unregister, view create, replace and
    register, and the transaction. A transaction's denial names the table the rule refused, by its own path.
  - Drop under a legal hold: `ObjectRoutes.drop` refuses through `Guard.refuse` with reason `legal_hold`.
  - Purge claim under a legal hold: `LifecycleRoutes.claimPurge` refuses through the new `ManagementGuard.held`.
- Side effects: the drop and purge-claim hold refusals are now `AccessDenied`, not a `CatalogError`, so an idempotency
  key no longer records the drop's 403, as item 132 made the freeze's. An engine refusal is still recorded under its
  key, as before; a replay of it carries no refusal and is not audited again, since its first answer was.
- Tests, in the fast build: `GovernanceRefusalTest` (7, new; the real table and transaction routes, guard,
  authorizer, commit engine and rules over a store double, through Spring MVC): a commit, a transaction and a create
  under a freeze, a snapshot removal from a held table, an unregister the store finds frozen or held, and a drop the
  store finds held. Each checks the 403 body and then the one denial. `FrozenLifecycleRefusalTest` gained a purge
  claim the store finds held. `RulesTest` expects the two rules' reasons.
- Bites, in `bites/D18/`, all run with `bites/run.sh`: BITES, tree restored. Each test failed on its
  `denials()` assertion, after its 403 assertions had passed:
  - `dr-freeze-commit-refusal-unaudited` (`Rules.drFreeze` without the reason): commit, transaction and create
    tests, 3 of 3 failed.
  - `legal-hold-commit-refusal-unaudited` (`Rules.retentionHold` without the reason): the snapshot removal test,
    1 of 1.
  - `dr-freeze-unregister-refusal-unaudited`, `legal-hold-unregister-refusal-unaudited` (the engine's unregister
    refusal without it): each unregister test, 1 of 1.
  - `commit-engine-refusal-unaudited` (`Guard.refused` publishes nothing): every engine test, 6 of 6.
  - `legal-hold-drop-refusal-unaudited` (the drop throws `AccessDenied` without publishing): the drop test, 1 of 1.
  - `legal-hold-purge-claim-refusal-unaudited` (`ManagementGuard.held` publishes nothing): the purge claim test,
    1 of 1.
- Bites recorded again, with the same break against the new code: `bites/M7/dr-freeze-never-rejects.patch` (BITES,
  1 of 1, `RulesTest`) and `bites/M5/commit-unguarded.patch` (it passes `git apply --check`; its test,
  `SecurityGateTest`, is heavy and has not run here).
- Not here, found while doing this: three more 403s that publish no denial. A namespace drop refused by a legal hold
  (`NamespaceRoutes`, `Http.forbidden`). A snapshot removal refused by the minimum snapshot age
  (`Rules.snapshotMinimumAge`, `Rejection.forbidden`). An unregister the store refuses as flagged (`CommitEngine`,
  `Reason.FLAGGED`). The view routes and the table register route call `Guard.refused` but have no test of their own.
