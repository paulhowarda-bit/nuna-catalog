Found by Atelier on 2026-11-26 while porting the console's header into her prototype (item 299), and not yet checked
in the console. The console's `style.css` carries the same two rules that went wrong in the prototype:

- The phone rule for `.ref-menu` (`left: 0` from the chip, `min-width: 300px`) was written when the chip sat in the page
  head. With the chip mid-header, an open Reading menu widened the prototype's page to 412 px at a 361 px frame and to
  498 px at 390.
- The names' 12ch caps share the room on a phone, and beside a long catalog name at 361 px the Reading chip's "main"
  ended in an ellipsis ("m…").

The prototype's fixes, in its phone block under the comments "main is four letters…" and "the chip sits mid-header…":
`main` keeps its width and the catalog name gives way, and the menu hangs from the header, 12 px in, `width:
min(380px, 100% - 24px)`.

Done when a browser test shows, at 361 and 390 px frames with a long catalog name, that the page does not scroll
sideways while the Reading menu is open and that "main" shows whole, or shows either was never wrong in the console.

Done (2026-11-26, backlog-300): both were wrong in the console, and both now take Atelier's rules from her prototype.

- Beside a long catalog name at a 361 px frame, the Reading chip showed 13 of main's 29 px ("m…"). Now main keeps its
  width on a phone and the catalog's name gives way.
- The open Reading menu, hung from the chip's right edge with a 300 px minimum, spanned 154 to 454 px in a 346 px window
  at 361. On a phone it now hangs from the header, 12 px in, `width: min(380px, 100% - 24px)`.
- Checked by `ConsoleBrowserTest#onAPhoneTheReadingChipShowsMainWholeAndItsMenuOpensInTheWindow`, at 361 and 390 px
  frames (emulated, as in item 297's test). The test's catalog is "trading", so it writes a 25-character name into the
  catalog picker, as a long warehouse name would show. It asserts the header fits, no part of it overlaps another,
  main shows whole while the catalog's name ends in an ellipsis, and the open menu lies inside the window with the
  page not scrolling sideways.
