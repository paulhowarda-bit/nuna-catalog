With maintenance off in a catalog's policy, the empty queue (`queue.js` `emptyQueue`) and Overview's "Nothing queued"
say so but give no way to turn it on. Atelier's words table for backlog 202 (`docs/design/console/words-table-2026-11-23.md`, "Not words" 1) asks for an
**Edit the catalog's policy** link after "Maintenance is off in {catalog}'s policy…", for people who may modify the
catalog.

Done looks like: both empty states end with that link, opening the catalog's maintenance policy editor, shown only to
callers who may modify the catalog; a heavy browser test for both, and a bite.

**What was done.** `maintenance.js` `editPolicyLink`: after "Maintenance is off in {catalog}'s policy…" on the
empty queue and Overview's "Nothing queued", an **Edit the catalog's policy** link opens the catalog's policy editor,
drawn only when the caller's groups hold modify on the catalog itself (what setting its policy needs; the server
still decides the save). Tests in `ConsoleHealthTest`: nell, who may not modify the catalog, sees no link; new
`maintenanceOffOffersTheCatalogsPolicyToThoseWhoMayModifyIt` answers nell's grants in the page with modify on the
catalog (a real catalog grant would show her every table, the assessed ones among them, and so no "off") and opens
the editor from both pages. Bite `bites/UI/console-policy-link-for-everyone.patch`. Not checked live: no demo catalog
has maintenance off with tables in it.
