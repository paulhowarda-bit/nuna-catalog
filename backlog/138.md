Effort: S, one console panel over a route that exists (`GET /management/v1/dr/watermarks`), with a browser test and a bite

The redesign's Health page (D45; `docs/design/console/prototype.html`, the health template, h2 "DR replication") has a
DR replication panel: the tables behind their watermark, the most versions behind (linking to that table) and the
longest lag. Item 124 left it out: "Left out, because no route serves them: the DR replication and Store panels on
Health" (`backlog/124.md`). Paul approved building it on 2026-11-22.

Evidence:
- The route exists since item 28: `GET /management/v1/dr/watermarks` (`DrRoutes.readout`), platform operators only,
  by id, 50 a page by default and at most 200, each table or view behind with `pointer-version`, `watermark-version`
  (null when it has none), `versions-behind` and `lag-seconds`.
- With no verifier report, every active table and view is listed: `listDrBehind` lists those whose watermark is below
  the pointer "or which have none", and `DrState.behind()` counts every version when there is none. A deployment with
  no DR store, as the demo, or before the verifier's first pass (item 92 still holds its settings), would show every
  table as behind by all its versions, its lag from its first commit. A count of those is not a DR lag.
- The readout cannot tell "no verifier has ever reported" from "these tables have not been verified yet": a table at
  its watermark is not listed. What it can say truly is which listed tables have a watermark.

What done looks like:
- Health shows a DR replication panel to platform operators. Anyone else is refused the Health page already
  (`/health/catalog`), so never reaches it; a refusal of the readout itself leaves the panel out, not a refusal box.
- The panel pages through the readout, 200 a page, at most 5 pages (1,000 tables and views): one journal read a row,
  so a bigger estate would make each Health visit read thousands. Past that it says "at least 1,000" and that the
  figures are of the first 1,000 by id.
- Tables with a watermark are counted as behind it; the most versions behind (linking to the table's page) and the
  longest lag are theirs. Tables with none are counted apart, as never verified.
- When no listed table has a watermark, the panel says no DR verifier has reported on them instead of a count.
- When none is listed, it says no table or view is behind its watermark.
- A browser test (`heavy`, a new class, not `ConsoleBrowserTest`): the no-verifier wording, then the counts, the most
  behind with its link and the lag; a non-operator sees no DR panel. A bite for the no-verifier rule.
