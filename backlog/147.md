Paul approved it on 2026-11-22. In the console's namespace and catalog **Contents**
(`catalog-server/src/main/resources/nuna/ui/browse.js`, `contentsTable`), a table row shows Format, Snapshots and
Last commit (item 137), but a child namespace's row shows a dash in all three. The prototype
(`docs/design/console/prototype.html`, `nsRow`) draws a namespace row's totals over every table beneath it
(`tablesUnder`, the whole subtree): the name with "· N tables", the sum of their snapshots, and the newest commit
among them with the table's path below the row ("20 Nov 17:30 · eu.trades"); Format stays a dash.

Evidence:
- `browse.js`, `contentsTable`: the namespace rows end `h('td', {class: 'num snaps'}, muted('–')), h('td', {class:
  'last'}, muted('–'))`.
- Item 137, "Left over": a namespace row's sum of its tables' snapshots and newest commit, which the prototype draws.
- No route gives them: `GET .../namespaces/{namespace}/tables` (item 137) gives one namespace's own tables only, and one
  console call per child row would be a call per row and per level below it.

What done looks like:
- One read, paged with the same bounds as item 137's, gives each child namespace of a namespace (or of the catalog)
  its totals over the tables beneath it that the caller may describe: how many, their snapshots summed, and the newest
  commit with that table's path. Tables the caller may not describe are not counted. No metadata file is read, no
  table loaded, no audit record written per table. A walk cut short by the bound says so.
- Contents shows them on each namespace row, as the prototype draws them.
- A fast route test (store double) for the totals and what is left out; `ConsoleBrowserTest` (heavy) for the row; a
  bite for a table counted that the caller may not describe; the two bites of item 137 still apply.
- `docs/observability/namespace-tables.md` describes the read.
