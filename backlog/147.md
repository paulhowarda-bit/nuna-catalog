What was done:

- **The read.** `GET /management/v1/catalogs/{catalog}/namespaces` (the catalog's top namespaces) and
  `GET /management/v1/catalogs/{catalog}/namespaces/{namespace}/namespaces` (a namespace's children), beside item
  137's route in `NamespaceTablesRoutes`, documented in `docs/observability/namespace-tables.md`. One row per child,
  by name: `tables` (how many active tables beneath it the caller may describe), `snapshots` (their projections'
  counts summed), `complete`, and `newest-commit` (version, `committed-at`, principal, end user, engine, and `table`,
  its levels below the child). No store change: the same `readPointer` and `readJournal` as item 137, a table at a
  time, plus the namespace and table listings of the walk.
- **The whole subtree**, as the prototype's `nsRow` counts it (`tablesUnder`), not only the child's direct tables.
- **Who sees what.** A table the caller may not describe is not counted, and not audited. A child the caller holds
  nothing on, above or in is no row. The parent is guarded as item 137's route guards a namespace (404
  `NoSuchNamespaceException`, audited as `listNamespaceTotals`); the catalog's top level needs something held in the
  catalog.
- **Bounds.** `pageSize` 100 by default, 1 to 200; a page looks at 1,000 tables and namespaces at most. A child whose
  walk would pass that waits for the next page, unless it is the page's first row, whose totals then cover what it
  looked at with `"complete": false`.
- **Contents** (`browse.js`): one paged read for all the namespace rows of the catalog's or a namespace's Contents.
  A namespace row shows "· N tables" after its name, its snapshots summed, and the newest commit's time (full date on
  hover) with the table's path below it (`xetra.quotes`); Format stays a dash. A walk cut short shows `412+ tables`
  and `9,412+`, with a title saying so. A namespace with no table counted shows dashes.
- **Tests.** `NamespaceTablesRoutesTest` 14 tests (7 new), Java only, passing. `ConsoleBrowserTest
