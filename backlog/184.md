## Done, 2026-11-23 (claude/console-wayfinding-d47d6a)

`GET /management/v1/catalogs` now says what the caller may do (`UiRoutes`): each catalog's `may` has `select`,
`modify`, `manage-grants` and `manage-tags`, true when one of the subject's groups holds a grant implying it on the
catalog or anything in it (grant admins hold `manage-grants` everywhere), and the answer's `may` has
`read-audit-feed` (select on the audit tables' catalog, decided by the authorizer, which audits nothing) and
`operate` (a platform operator). It is read from the caller's own grants and groups, so asking writes no denial.
`CatalogListTest` (fast) has two new cases.

The console keeps it (`access.js`) and draws: Overview and Maintenance for those who read data, Audit for audit readers
and grant managers, Health for platform operators; an object's Audit tab and the Governance page's Grants tab for those
who manage the catalog's grants; a Grants tab with only "Your groups' grants" for anyone else; label and hold buttons
for `manage-tags`, Release likewise, Freeze and Lift for operators. A link to a page or tab the caller cannot use says
so in plain words ("Audit is open to auditors and to those who manage lakehouse's grants. Your groups: …") without
asking the catalog. Overview no longer asks for the latency or audit figures of a caller who may not read them, which
wrote a denial on every visit. Landing: Overview for data readers, Audit for auditors and grant admins, Health for
operators. `ConsoleBrowserTest.eachRoleSeesWhatItsGroupsCanUseAndLandsWhereItsWorkIs` (heavy) checks the tester, a
grant admin and an operator; three heavy tests that expected a refusal or a button now expect neither.

Not yet checked live: the demo runs the server from before this change, so its catalog list has no `may`. Left: a
grant admin with no data lands on the first catalog's Audit (`engines` in the demo) until they pick another, since no
table count ranks catalogs for them; refusals that still happen lead with plain words in item 188.
