Paul, 2026-11-26, asked whether to give the CDC source databases and CloudBeaver volumes: "Yes, add them". Told
that they never had any (D52 left them without, and every cdc started the leg anew) and offered volumes only while
the leg runs, or the leg keeping its state like the rest of the demo: "yes option 2".

What is wrong: the CDC leg's three databases (backoffice, oracle, cockroach) and CloudBeaver's workspace keep their
data in their containers. `cdc stop` and `down` delete the databases with the connectors' offsets and topics, and
each `cdc` deletes the history tables and starts again from the SQL files; CloudBeaver rebuilds its users and
connections at every start and loses what people saved.

Done when:

- Each database and CloudBeaver keeps its data on a volume claim in the namespace, which `wipe` deletes.
- `cdc stop` and `down` stop the connectors (keeping their offsets), delete the leg's pods and keep the claims,
  topics, consumer groups and history tables; the next `cdc` starts the databases from their volumes and the
  connectors carry on from where they stopped, without snapshotting again.
- `cdc reset` deletes the leg as `cdc stop` did before, its claims too, so the next `cdc` starts anew.
- CloudBeaver's kept connections get the people's current tokens and passwords at each start.
- Checked live: a first `cdc`, `cdc stop`, `cdc` again (no new snapshot rows; a change made then reaches its history
  table), and a CloudBeaver sign-in after its pod is replaced.


What was done (2026-11-26, demo-volumes): volume claims for the three CDC databases and CloudBeaver's workspace;
Oracle's ready-made database copied onto its volume by an init container and `oracle.sql` run once per volume;
CockroachDB's data and certificates on its volume; `cdc stop` and `down` pause the leg, `cdc` carries on (configmap
`cdc-begun`), `cdc reset` starts anew; CloudBeaver updates kept connections. Checked live: PostgreSQL and Oracle
resume without repeating rows, CockroachDB repeats the changes made just before a stop (documented), CloudBeaver
keeps its workspace. `status/2026-11-26-demo-volumes.md` has the details.
