## Done, 2026-11-23 (claude/console-wayfinding-d47d6a)

No server change was needed: each journal entry the metadata log answers carries its `updates`, and an `add-snapshot`
names the snapshot it added, so the console pairs a snapshot with its commit (`table.js` `snapshotCommits`: the log
read a page at a time, newest first, until every snapshot asked for is found or 200 commits are read). A table's
Overview adds "by lake via lake-writer-demo" under its current snapshot (`#current-by`); Snapshots has a By column,
filled in place once the log is read and blank for a snapshot whose commit the journal no longer holds or lies further
back. Contents reads "12:49 by lake" on a table's row and "12:49 in rates" on a namespace's (`browse.js`). Checked
live in the demo as `ana` through the swap on `markets.fx.rates` (Overview, Snapshots' three rows) and the
`markets` and `markets.fx` Contents. `ConsoleBrowserTest#contentsShowEachTablesFormatSnapshotsAndLastCommit` (heavy)
checks all three; bite `console-snapshot-author-unpaired`.
