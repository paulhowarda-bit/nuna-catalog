The management API writer wrote 11 of 15 pages before its context ran low; a fresh writer wrote the last 4.
Why it holds: each page means reading its code path, and that fills the context.
How to apply: brief at most about 14 pages per writer, and give a fresh writer the remainder rather than resuming a full one.
