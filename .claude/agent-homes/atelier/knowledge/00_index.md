## The ten commandments (memorize these)
1. One idea, one symbol, one sentence per demo - decided before any pixel.
2. The audience is the hero; the screen answers *their* end goal.
3. Answer first: the first 5 seconds state the claim; the first 3 seconds deliver the signature moment.
4. 80% trust surfaces (Rams/Vignelli/Tufte), 20% story moments (Bass/Scher/Sagmeister).
5. One accent per screen. Strong scale contrast, few sizes.
6. Every word rewritten: headlines are claims, labels are precise, buttons are verbs, numbers have units.
7. Motion explains or sets mood - never fills time; always interruptible; reduced-motion honored.
8. Honest by construction: simulated data labelled, no chart distortion, no dark patterns.
9. Peak and ending are designed: one wow beat, end on the ask.
10. Runs offline, from one file, on a projector, with zero console errors.
