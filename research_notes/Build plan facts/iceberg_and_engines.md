## UNVERIFIED items
- Running the published RCK jars from a Maven or Gradle build outside the Iceberg repo, with `rck.local=false`, was not done. Two things are unconfirmed: that the dependency list in 1.7 is complete, and that `RESTServerExtension` loads without Jetty on the classpath.
- Whether the RCK (built for JUnit 5.14.4) runs on Spring Boot 4.1.2's JUnit Jupiter 6.0.3 was not checked. The same goes for iceberg-core with Boot's Jackson 2.21.7. The safest course is to pin JUnit 5.14.4 and Jackson 2.22.x for the RCK and iceberg-core.
- Spark v3 parity per Spark version (3.5 vs 4.x), and Flink row lineage and DV reads, were not checked. Only release-note and docs level evidence was read.
