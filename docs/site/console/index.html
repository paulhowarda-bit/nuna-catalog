The console

The console is the web interface the server serves at /ui/. This page answers, for anyone who signs in to it, how it is laid out: the header, the rail of pages, Go to a path, the keys and the phone layout, and which pages your groups let you see.

Who sees it

Anyone the server accepts a token from can sign in. The console draws only the pages, tabs and buttons your groups can use, from what GET /management/v1/catalogs says your groups hold in each catalog. The server still decides every request itself: a page you reach by a link and may not use shows the server's refusal.

The catalog picker lists only the catalogs where one of your groups holds a grant on the catalog or on anything in it. A grant admin and a platform operator see every catalog. A person who holds nothing sees no catalog, and the console shows No catalog to show with Your groups hold nothing in any catalog.

A page opened by a link that your groups cannot use shows why instead of its content:

  • Grants in catalog are open to those who manage its grants.
  • Audit is open to auditors and to those who manage catalog's grants.
  • Service health is open to platform operators.

Each is followed by Your groups: groups. Ask a grant admin if your work needs it.

The first page in the rail is your home, and the console opens on it. It is the Overview for a person who reads the audit feed, operates the platform and reads data. Otherwise it is the Audit page for an auditor, the Service page for a platform operator, the Overview for someone who reads data, and the Catalog page for everyone else.

The rail

The rail, labelled Console, lists Overview, Catalog, Branches and tags, Maintenance, Governance, Grants, Audit and Service, each shown only as Who sees it says. Its foot reads Press / to go to a path, ? for keys.

  • The Overview page: how the catalog's tables stand, last night's maintenance and the day's figures.
  • The Catalog page: the catalog's contents and its declared properties. From it you open namespace, table and view pages.
  • The Branches and tags page: the catalog's refs.
  • The Maintenance page: tonight's queue, the nights, the policy.
  • The Governance page: classification, holds, freezes, regulated areas.
  • The Grants page: who holds what, granting and revoking.
  • The Audit page: every record in the catalog.
  • The Service page: the deployment's health.

When the console starts, it lands you in the catalog you last used in this browser. Otherwise it picks, for an auditor, the catalog with the most audit records in the last day; then the catalog with the most tables you may read; then the first. It lands you in the audit tables' own catalog only when that is the only one.

Go to a path

Go to a path finds a namespace, table or view in any catalog you may see. Its field reads Part of a name in any catalog, or a full dotted path, and its foot says ↑ ↓ to move, Enter to open. Matches every word you type, in any order, across every catalog you may see: what you opened lately first. A full dotted path opens in the catalog shown.

  • With the field empty, it lists what you opened lately (eight per person, kept in this browser) and the tables queued for tonight, under Opened lately, then queued for tonight, Opened lately or Queued for tonight, neediest first.
  • As you type, it searches every catalog's names and shows at most nine matches (First 9 matches). Every word you type must appear in the path. Each match is marked namespace, view or with its queue position, and in catalog when it is in another catalog.
  • A full dotted path that is not listed shows Open path, marked look up; Enter looks it up in the catalog shown.

The phone layout

At 760 px wide or less, the rail becomes a drawer that the menu button opens. The drawer adds Go to a path, the theme switch, a Signed in as … line and Sign out. Wider than that, the rail stays on the left and folds to icons.

Answers and refusals

Every change and every refusal shows the server's answer. A refusal (403) is titled Refused by the catalog; any other error The catalog answered with an error. The server's message follows word for word, and for a refusal The refusal is recorded in the audit. Under Technical details are the status and error type, the X-Request-ID (or No request id came back), the time and Copy request id. A request that never reached the server shows The request did not reach the catalog; a fault in the console itself shows The console failed to show this.

A link to a page that does not exist shows Nothing at this path: "It may have been renamed or dropped, or the link was cut short." Old links to pages that have moved still open their new place.
