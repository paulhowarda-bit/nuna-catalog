The Maintenance page

The Maintenance page answers, for data owners and platform operators, which tables the maintenance worker will compact tonight and why, what the last 30 nights did, how the catalog's policy is set, and what the worker still needs before it can maintain the catalog.

Who sees it

The rail shows Maintenance when your groups may read data in the catalog, and its badge counts the tables queued for tonight. Every list counts only the tables you may read; jobs on other tables are drawn without a name. Editing the policy needs modify on the catalog; Compact a table now needs modify somewhere in it. The setup checklist appears only to those who may read it (a grant held on the catalog itself, a grant admin or a platform operator). Worker scans is for platform operators.

The head

The title says how the catalog stands: the setup steps left, Maintenance is off in catalog's policy: nothing will be compacted or expired., N tables queued for tonight. or Every table you may read in catalog is in shape. "The worker compacts the neediest first, in its cleanup window. Only the tables you may read are listed; each one's assessment is on its Maintenance tab." A pill gives Assessed HH:MM.

Compact a table now opens Compact a table now: "Choose a table in catalog that your groups may compact. Tonight's queue comes first." Pick from Queued for tonight or Other tables you may compact, or Another table, by its path… ("Its namespace's levels and its name, joined by dots."), then Next and Compact now. Frozen tables cannot be chosen. The table page describes the request.

What the worker needs

The panel What the worker needs to maintain catalog appears until every step is done, with N of 4 done as of the worker's last scan:

StepWhoAction in the console
1. Write accessA grant adminGrant write to group, or Ask a grant admin.
2. Storage settingsA platform operator, in the worker's settingsWhat to add
3. Store permissionA platform operator, in the storeWhat to apply or Open Worker storage
4. Maintenance policySomeone with modify on the catalogEdit the catalog's policy

Each step is Done, Missing, Refused, Not known, Waits on step 2 or No answer. Others get Copy a note for an operator. With no report: "No maintenance worker has reported, so the catalog can't check this." "The catalog can't change the worker's settings or the store's policies: the worker's keys stay out of the catalog, and a catalog that could set store policies could undo the one that keeps delete from it."

The last 30 nights

Four figures, each with a 30-night line: Tables compacted, last night, Small files merged, Snapshots expired and Unreferenced files deleted. They are the whole deployment's figures.

Tonight's queue

Tonight's queue, "equality deletes first, then the highest score", with #, Table, Why, Score, Small files, Deletes / data and Last compaction. Filter by All, Equality deletes, Small files or Deletes. Empty: Nothing queued for tonight, with why, and "Tables you may not read are not counted here."

Last night

The night's jobs, slot by slot, as on the Overview.

Policy

Policy for catalog lists each key with its Value and Set by. "Needs modify on catalog. Namespaces and tables can set their own keys." Edit the catalog's policy opens a dialog: "Keys set here apply to every table in catalog that does not set its own. A guardrail, a hold or a freeze, still wins. Leave a key empty to use the built-in value." Select Save the policy. With every key empty the policy is removed (Removed the policy of catalog). "The worker reads the policy at its next scan."

The keys are Snapshot expiry, Keep snapshots for, Keep at least, Orphan removal, Remove orphans older than, Compaction, Target file size, Fewest input files, Delete-file share, Manifest rewrite and Fewest manifests. Set a catalog's maintenance policy gives each default.

Worker scans

For platform operators, the worker's recent scans, as on the Service page.
