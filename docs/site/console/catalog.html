The Catalog page

The Catalog page answers, for anyone who may see a catalog, what it holds that your groups may see and how it was declared: its storage profile, location and encryption.

Who sees it

Everyone who may see the catalog. The catalog picker lists a catalog only where one of your groups holds a grant on it or on something in it; grant admins and platform operators see every catalog. Listing a namespace needs describe on it, so the page lists only what your groups may see.

The head and the tree

The head shows the catalog's name and catalog, then how many namespaces it has and N tables queued for tonight or nothing you may read is queued for tonight. Copy warehouse copies the warehouse name an engine uses, and Copy link copies the page's address.

On the left, the tree lists the namespaces, tables and views, opened level by level. Filter names in the tree narrows it to matching names among the levels already open. Its key marks equality deletes, small files or deletes, held, DR freeze and regulated, and, when you read through a branch, changed on @ref. When nothing open matches: "No open namespace in catalog holds a matching name. Open more of the tree, or use Go to a path."

Contents

The Contents tab lists the top-level namespaces, with these columns (an empty column is left out):

Name and Kind
The name, and namespace, table or view. A namespace shows how many tables it holds, with a "+" when the count stopped at the first 1,000 tables and namespaces beneath it.
Format
The table's Iceberg format version, such as v2.
Tonight's queue
N queued.
Governance
Held, Frozen, regulated or Closed until classified.
Snapshots
How many snapshots the table keeps.
Last commit
HH:MM by who, or by person via principal when an engine committed for someone. On a namespace, the latest commit beneath it and the table it was in.

The note beneath reads "Only what your groups may see is listed. Open a table for its maintenance, access and history." When nothing is listed: No namespace in this catalog, "Or nothing your groups may see. If you expected something here, ask a grant admin."

Read through a branch or tag, the table's figures are as the ref has it: the columns become Last commit here and On @ref, and the note says "Format, snapshots and last commit are as @ref has each table. Open one to read it through @ref."

Properties

The Properties tab shows the catalog as the catalog was declared: Name, Legal entity, Storage profile (its name and kind), Endpoint (or the provider's default), Region, Table root, Allowed prefixes, Encryption (SSE-KMS, with a key of its own or the bucket's default) and KMS key set.

Nothing here can be changed in the console: "Set when the deployment was configured; they can't be changed here. The catalog's owner and classification are under Governance, and how its tables are compacted, expired and cleaned up is its maintenance policy. Storage keys and roles are never shown." The two links open the catalog's own governance and its maintenance policy.
