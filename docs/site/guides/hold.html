Place and lift a legal hold

Place a legal hold on a catalog, namespace, table or view so that nothing under it can be deleted, and release it when the matter ends.

For data stewards Needs manage-tags on the object Audited

Before you begin

  • Your group holds manage-tags on the object or above it.
  • You have the reason as it must be recorded, such as the matter it is for. The hold carries it for as long as it stands, and the audit trail keeps it after.
  • For the curl examples, NUNA and TOKEN are set as Use the APIs with curl describes.

While a hold stands, nothing under it can be dropped or purged, snapshot expiry and orphan removal stop, and commits that remove snapshots are refused. Commits that add data still succeed, and compaction still runs. On a view, the hold stops it being dropped or purged; its definition can still be replaced. Governance lists every block.

Place a hold

  1. Open the table, view or namespace, and select its Access tab.

    To hold a path you do not want to open, select Governance in the rail, then Holds and freezes, then Place a hold on a path.

  2. Select Place a hold.

    On a namespace the button is Place a hold here. The dialog Place a hold on fills opens.

  3. Enter the reason in Reason, as it will be recorded.

  4. Select Place the hold.

    The dialog confirms with Held: and the object's name, and the hold appears in the Legal holds panel.

Post the reason to the object's holds. A catalog's are at /management/v1/catalogs/trading/holds, a namespace's at …/namespaces/equities/holds, a view's at …/views/view/holds.

curl -sS -X POST "$NUNA/management/v1/catalogs/trading/namespaces/equities/tables/fills/holds" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"reason": "Matter 2026-114: trade surveillance review"}'

It answers 201 with the hold. Keep its id: you release the hold by it.

{
  "id": "acb5a6a8-9603-443b-a7a6-f5e4903c8953",
  "level": "table:…",
  "reason": "Matter 2026-114: trade surveillance review",
  "placed-by": "…",
  "placed-at": "2026-11-26T15:17:19.210Z"
}

The hold is standing

The catalog's holds list it under standing, with its reason, who placed it and when. In the console it is on the Holds and freezes tab of the Governance page, in Standing.

curl -sS "$NUNA/management/v1/catalogs/trading/governance/holds" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN"

Release a hold

Release a hold on the level it was placed on. Releasing it lets the worker expire snapshots and remove files there again, and lets the objects be dropped and purged.

  1. Select Release on the hold's row.

    The row is in the Legal holds panel of any object under the hold, or in Standing on the Holds and freezes tab. The dialog Release a hold opens.

  2. Select Release the hold.

    The dialog confirms with Released. The hold moves to Lifted and released.

Delete the hold by its id, on the object it was placed on. It answers 204.

curl -sS -X DELETE "$NUNA/management/v1/catalogs/trading/namespaces/equities/tables/fills/holds/acb5a6a8-9603-443b-a7a6-f5e4903c8953" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN"

Each placement and release leaves an audit record of type nuna.catalog.hold, with the action (PLACED or RELEASED), the hold's id, the object, the reason and who did it. The catalog's holds list shows released holds for 30 days.

If it fails

What you see, why, and what to do
What you seeWhyWhat to do
400 Missing field reason: a non-empty stringNo reason was sent. The console says Write the reason: the hold carries it for as long as it stands.Enter the reason.
400 Field reason is longer than 1000 charactersThe reason is too long.Shorten it and point to the matter's own record.
403 subject holds no privilege that gives manage-tags for …Your groups hold no manage-tags on the object or above it.Ask a grant admin for the grant, or ask a data steward.
404 No hold … on this tableThe hold is not on this object: it was placed on a level above, or has been released.Release it on the level it was placed on; the holds list names it.
