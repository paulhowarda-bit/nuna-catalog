Kafka, metrics and traces in the demo

Look at the demo's Kafka topics and connectors in kafbat, and turn on the metrics stack to see nuna's dashboards, alert rules and traces in Grafana.

Demo only For platform operators

Before you begin

  • The demo is running (Start, stop and reset the demo).
  • Kafka runs only while the brokerage world (gen, with kafka on) or the CDC leg runs. Until then port 8182 answers 502.
  • The metrics stack needs 1,472 MiB free on the cluster. It fits beside gen and kafka, or beside ss, but not beside all three.

Kafka in kafbat

kafbat on https://localhost:8182 is Kafka's web page. People sign in through Keycloak. It shows:

  • the brokerage world's brokerage.* topics and its sink's control topic brokerage-lake-control;
  • Kafka Connect's own connect-* topics;
  • while the CDC leg runs, Debezium's change topics, the sinks' control topics and Oracle's schema history;
  • once the engine matrix has run, its topic m8-kafka-events and its sink's control topic;
  • under Kafka Connect, cluster connect: each connector's status, tasks and config, with restart, pause and resume for oscar.

oscar administers kafbat and reads its audit log. ana, gary and stella administer topics, but only read the catalog's (nuna.*), Kafka Connect's, the brokerage world's and those starting with _.

Turn on metrics and traces

  1. Turn the switch on.

    demo/demo.sh metrics

    It starts four pods and points the server's and the maintenance worker's telemetry.endpoint at the first, which rolls the server to a new pod.

  2. Open Grafana.

    Go to https://localhost:8188 and sign in through Keycloak. The home page is the catalog's dashboard.

  3. Follow a trace.

    Select a chart's exemplar point to open the trace of that request in Tempo, or search traces in Explore with data source Tempo.

The dashboards have data

Once the server has served requests, every panel of nuna catalog has data except the failure panels, which stay empty until something fails. Loading a table that does not exist shows in Failed loads.

What the switch runs

PodWhat it doesMemory asked
OpenTelemetry collectorTakes OTLP over HTTP from the server and the worker. Sends metrics to Prometheus and traces to Tempo, and drops logs.64 MiB
PrometheusTakes the metrics over OTLP and loads the alert rules in deploy/grafana/rules. Keeps 7 days.256 MiB
TempoKeeps the traces for 2 days.128 MiB
GrafanaThe three dashboards in deploy/grafana/dashboards, in folder nuna, and Explore.256 MiB

The dashboards are nuna catalog, nuna maintenance worker and nuna client reports. No Alertmanager runs, so alerts show on Grafana's Alerting page and in the series ALERTS, and notify no one.

While the switch is on the server and the worker record every trace, and the collector keeps every trace with an error, every trace slower than 300 ms and a tenth of the rest. A maintenance worker's scan is one trace, with the server's calls in it.

Prometheus takes OTLP with names translated by UnderscoreEscapingWithSuffixes, native histograms off, a 30-minute out-of-order window, and exemplars kept.

Who may do what in Grafana

oscar and presenter administer Grafana; ana, gary and stella view. Grafana's own admin signs in at https://localhost:8188/login?disableAutoLogin=true with the password in ~/.nuna-demo/secrets/grafana-admin.

Turn it off

demo/demo.sh metrics stop

The series, traces and Grafana's database stay on their volumes through metrics stop and down; wipe deletes them.
