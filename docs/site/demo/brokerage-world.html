The brokerage world

The brokerage world is a simulated US broker-dealer whose generator writes a firm's trading, ledger and clearing data into the demo's catalog lakehouse as it happens, through Kafka, SingleStore or both.

Demo only

What it is

A firm with 1,000 customer accounts (BROKERAGE_ACCOUNTS=1000) that books every financial transaction through a posting engine as journal lines, and clears its own trades through NSCC's Continuous Net Settlement (CNS). It runs while the switch gen is on, as Deployment brokerage. Its account numbers, securities and other identifiers are synthetic, and the same seed (BROKERAGE_SEED=20261122) produces the same world.

Its events keep the real clock of New York: a trading day's events fall between 07:00 and 17:00 there, so the world is quiet overnight, at weekends and on market holidays, and its log says quiet until time. When the generator starts it writes three catch-up days (BROKERAGE_CATCH_UP_DAYS=3) and today's earlier hours at once, then sends each event at its time.

How it works

demo/demo.sh gen starts SingleStore if ss is on and Kafka if kafka is, then runs the job brokerage-bootstrap. As user brokerage, the job makes the world's namespaces, tables and views in lakehouse, and its Kafka topics while Kafka is on. gen then sets their governance as stella, starts Kafka Connect, registers the sink or the change feeds, and starts the generator last.

Through Kafka

The generator writes plain JSON to nineteen topics named brokerage.area.feed, such as brokerage.ledger.journal-lines. Kafka Connect's Iceberg sink brokerage-lake, writing as user cdc, sends each record to the table its lake_table field names and commits every 15 seconds. The sink neither makes nor changes tables; the bootstrap job made them.

Through SingleStore

The generator writes the orders to the SingleStore table brokerage.orders, and the order management system's rows to brokerage.order_events, routes and executions. Two change feeds copy them into brokerage.trading every 15 seconds (SingleStore in the demo).

Before a way goes off, the script stops the generator and waits up to three minutes until everything sent that way is in the lake; if it is not, that way stays on. The generator then starts on its new route without sending anything twice.

Clearing and custody

Each day's fills net per security into one CNS obligation that settles at 08:00 the next business day (cns_obligations, settlements). Every short sale is accepted under a locate (locates, joined to the order's acceptance by locate_id). At 17:00 each day the world writes the stock record: every position by owner and by location (DTC, stock borrowed, CNS pending, failed to deliver), which balances.

One break is seeded. On the world's third business day or later, the first net delivery that holds a customer short sale fails for that sale's shares (fails), ages a day, and is closed out at 09:30 on the next settlement day under Reg SHO Rule 204(a) by a purchase for the firm's own account.

The CDC leg loads the clearing tables into CockroachDB database custody as well (Change data capture in the demo).

Reconciliation views

Three views in brokerage.recon, in Trino's dialect, are empty when the books agree. Each reads only the days before today.

ViewCompares
stock_record_vs_ledgerThe stock record's customer positions with the ledger's share and short positions and the open lots.
executions_vs_fillsEach execution with its ledger fill.
cns_vs_fillsCNS with the net of the customers' fills, per security and trade date.

The seeded fail shows as one row of cns_vs_fills, on its close-out's trade date: the shares the firm bought and what it paid. In Trino:

SELECT * FROM lakehouse."brokerage.recon".cns_vs_fills;
SELECT * FROM lakehouse."brokerage.clearing".fails ORDER BY fail_event_id;

Limits

  • The generator asks for 256 MiB, Kafka Connect 512 MiB and the bootstrap job 384 MiB while it runs. cdc pauses the generator; up, rate and engines stop it and Kafka Connect while they run.
  • gen stop stops the generator, waits 20 seconds for the sink's last commit, then stops Kafka Connect, Kafka and kafbat unless the CDC leg runs. Kafka's topics stay, so the next gen catches up on the days it missed without sending what it sent before.
  • wipe deletes the topics with the lake, and the next up starts a new world. If Kafka alone lost its topics, the generator stops and says so.

Not documented yet: the columns of each table. The schemas are in the module demo-datagen (LakeSchemas) and in demo/singlestore/brokerage-feeds.sql.
