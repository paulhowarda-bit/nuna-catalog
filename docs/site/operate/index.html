Operate

Day-to-day running of nuna: what to watch, what runs at night, and what to do when something fails.

Read the first four pages once when you set nuna up: they say what the server and the maintenance worker report and how to collect it. Keep the troubleshooting page and the runbooks to hand for when something goes wrong.

  • Health checksThe probes Kubernetes uses, the health routes for platform operators, and the database self-check at start-up.
  • MetricsEvery metric the server and the maintenance worker export, with its type, unit, labels and meaning.
  • Traces, logs and the request idThe spans each request and each scan make, the JSON log lines, and following one request across the parts.
  • Dashboards and alertsThe three Grafana dashboards and the 29 Prometheus alert rules, how to install them and what each watches.
  • Maintenance nightsThe worker's schedule, the cleanup window, and reading a night's figures.
  • Table healthThe health score, its reasons, and what puts a table in tonight's compaction queue.
  • Run the audit pipelineThe audit log, the outbox relay, the Kafka copy, the archive and the audit tables writer, and checking each is current.
  • Back up and recoverWhat to back up, the DR watermark and verifier, and recovering in the DR region step by step.
  • ScalingReplicas, the database, the worker's compaction slots, and what the load tests measured.
  • Helm and kubectl recipesStatus, logs, restarts after a Secret change and rollbacks for each of the three releases.
  • TroubleshootingSymptom, cause and fix, grouped by part, with the messages as nuna prints them.
  • Alert runbooksOne section per alert rule: what it means, its impact, what to check and how to fix it.
